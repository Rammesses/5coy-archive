## 25 November 2026 - Videos, Personnel Jackets and Other Publications {#2026-11-25-videos-personnel-publications .whats-new}

A new [Videos](/videos) page, two new in-character sections - [Personnel Jackets](/section/personnel-jackets)
and [Other Publications](/section/other-publications) - and a batch of newly transcribed
out-of-character documents.

- [Videos](/videos) brings together 17 videos from the campaign, both in-character (including CNN
  news reports and footage shot on Altair IV) and out-of-character. Each video has its own page with
  notes and a transcript, and shows up on the pages of the missions and articles it relates to.
- [Personnel Jackets](/section/personnel-jackets) has 20 new jackets, each linked to the missions
  that marine attended.
- [Other Publications](/section/other-publications) has transcribed issues of CNN Newsline,
  Dispatches and The Voice.
- More out-of-character material has been transcribed, including the Dark Horizons flyer, the
  monster code of conduct, the radiation rules, a history of the Colonial War and the
  [Planetary Database](/article/out-of-character/planetary-Database). The prop design scans have
  been tidied up and indexed on their own page.

## 23 November 2026 - 22 more issues of Interstella! {#2026-11-23-interstella .whats-new}

[Interstella!](/section/interstella) has grown by 22 issues, covering 2492 to 2496, each
transcribed in full so the text can be read and searched on the page alongside the original scan.

## 22 November 2026 - More mission summaries and corrected dates {#2026-11-22-mission-summaries}

Summaries added for four more missions, including Procyon, Sidewinder and Pegasus. Mission dates
have been checked against the mission reports and Mexal's letters so that every mission sits on the
right weekend, and the "Related Articles" links now work in both directions between articles and
missions.

## 21 November 2026 - Mission summaries {#2026-11-21-mission-summaries .whats-new}

Mission pages can now show a summary of what happened, drawn from the archive's own reports and
letters. The first summaries cover Nova, Omaha, Marie Celesta, Ogre, Nightside, Intruder and
Redstorm. Cross-references between articles were also fixed, and the [Missions](/missions) page
tidied up.

## 16 May 2026 - Upgraded to .NET 10 {#2026-05-16-dotnet-10}

The site was upgraded to .NET 10 (via .NET 8 in March).

## 24 March 2026 - Visual refresh {#2026-03-24-visual-refresh}

A round of small visual improvements across the site.

## 18 March 2026 - Deep links fixed {#2026-03-18-deep-links}

Links straight to an article or mission page now work, rather than only links to the home page.

## October 2025 - Hosting and media changes {#2025-10-hosting}

The site's infrastructure is now defined as code, and PDFs are served through a new media
redirect.

## 29 August 2024 - PDF viewer {#2024-08-29-pdf-viewer}

PDFs can now be read in a viewer on the page, with a separate button to download them.

## 20 April 2024 - New loading screen {#2024-04-20-loading-screen}

A better loading screen while the archive starts up, and a subtle panning background behind the
menu.

## 5 February 2024 - In The Line Of Fire scans {#2024-02-05-itlof-pdfs}

Every issue of [In The Line Of Fire](/section/in-the-line-of-fire) now links to its original
scan.

## 24 January 2024 - Mission pages {#2024-01-24-mission-pages}

Each mission on the [Missions](/missions) list now has its own page, listing the articles that
report on it.

## August 2022 - Folding menu {#2022-08-menu}

The menu now folds sections away, making the archive easier to get around.

## June 2022 - Missions list {#2022-06-missions}

A new [Missions](/missions) page lists every known mission, and the site moved to .NET 6.

## March 2022 - Interstella! and In The Line Of Fire {#2022-03-interstella-itlof}

[Interstella!](/section/interstella) and [In The Line Of Fire](/section/in-the-line-of-fire) were
added to the In-Character section, and each page's title now matches the section or article being
viewed.

## February 2022 - The new archive goes live {#2022-02-new-archive}

The rebuilt archive replaced the old static website. Highlights included the complete
[Mexal's Letters Home](/section/mexals-letters), the [Marine's Handbook](/section/marines-handbook)
(basic training, scouting, sniper, patrolling, radio, comptech, medtech and officer/NCO courses),
the experimental station and exo-suit briefing notes, sections nested within sections, "related"
links between articles, and PDFs downloaded directly from storage.

## July 2021 - Rebuild begins {#2021-07-rebuild}

Work started on a new version of the archive as a Blazor web app, with the missions list,
menu, articles and markdown content all working.

## April 2020 - Miscellanea {#2020-04-miscellanea}

A [Miscellanea](/section/miscellanea) section was added, along with the missing Drive Wars II
document.

## October 2014 - The archive launches {#2014-10-launch}

The original 5-Coy CMC Archive went online, with its first articles, a whole In-Character section,
and the missions list.
