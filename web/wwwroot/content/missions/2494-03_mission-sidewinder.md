Nyytoda was a farming world of 3 feuding clans: the Grants, the Douglases and the Ploughers. Each had been on the planet for a generation or more, and each was set on wiping out the other 2. In March 1994 5 Coy took over a permanent base there from Major Flank's unit, along with a fresh intake of SPICE recruits to train. Officer Cadet Calvin's [report](/article/mission-reports/2494-03_mission-sidewinder) notes approvingly that the recruits' barracks were rather tidier than the regulars'.

The clans got in touch quickly. Amos Grant, who did the talking for the biggest family, wanted to renew the "understanding" he'd had with Major Flank. The Grants would leave the Marines alone if the Marines leaned on the other 2 families, with a few side deals, coffee included. He also offered livestock in exchange for a female Marine. His kinsman Bart turned up wearing a CMC screen and weapon that Flank had listed as destroyed. The Douglases and Ploughers came to complain of collaboration and had to be pulled apart in the mess. All 3 clans wanted their hostages back, and only the Grants had got theirs. The real fourth party turned out to be a drug company trading with the Grants. A 3-pronged raid took out its sentry gun and uplink, but the team that hit its drop zone was captured. In the negotiations that followed, the body the Marines were allowed to recover was booby-trapped, and Marine Bean was shot with a slug thrower in front of them before the prisoners came home. The company ambushed the dealers' shuttle landing, with Calvin collecting 3 broken ribs from a hostile's boot. The recovered supplies then blew up inside the base, though fortunately nobody was hurt.

Calvin's conclusion was that each clan would deal with any off-worlder who gave it an edge, which the drug dealers had spotted long before the Marines arrived. How much Major Flank had known, nobody could say, and the Douglas and Plougher hostages were never found. His suggested fix had a certain cunning: convince each clan separately that the other 2 were about to gang up on it, then offer machine parts and fuel for peace talks in orbit, under Marine escort. A month later 5 Coy were on Hal for [Mission 'Ogre' - April 2494 (Hal)](/mission/2494-04_mission-ogre).
