The orders came over the CMC Commnet with no issuing officer, no Commnet ID and no document reference, which Officer Cadet Calvin's [report](/article/mission-reports/2494-04_mission-ogre) points out the system shouldn't even have accepted. In April 2494 Major Harris took 5 Coy to Hal, a slave-owning world outside the UW, by civilian ship to a disused reserve base, posing as a reserve training unit of the 3rd Cohort of the 9th Legion. The local contact, known in the report only as "the Senator", had full authority to give the company orders. His story was that if the Marines quietly put down a rebellion for him, he would push Hal towards the UW and the Projectile Weapons Ban.

It soon became clear what kind of man they were working for. The first militiaman handed over to him was executed by his guards. A captured rebel who kept insisting the Senator was a traitor was shot for treason without trial, in front of Harris, Oslo and Calvin. Of 4 frightened runaway slaves the Marines had let go, the Senator brought 1 back and executed them in front of the barracks "as a warning". The Senator's own mole in the rebel ranks was tortured to death with a blade while his guards held off the Marines trying to intervene. Then Interstella's Krystal Spyers walked up with a squad of Hal regulars and picked out Harris by name. The UW representative arrived and admitted the real plan: make the Senator president so he'd bring Hal into the UW. She then ordered Spyers executed. Harris refused to act without a signed written order. He got one, with no charges on it, took Spyers outside, and she came back pronounced dead. She was back in print soon enough. With the Senator shooting every prisoner, the company stopped reviving downed enemy at all.

The military side was almost routine by comparison. A night raid on the Hal military comms relay was called off after leg injuries and a hailstorm, since Hal's weather could switch from clear sky to heavy snow in minutes. On the Sunday both the relay and the rebel leadership meeting were hit, and Calvin called a trip wire just before someone else set off the APD on the end of it. Then a battalion of Hal regulars surrounded the base, addressed the Marines as "CMC 5 Coy", gave the Senator 24 hours to leave the planet and asked the company to surrender. Having seen what the Senator did to prisoners, nobody fancied a fight on his behalf. The company destroyed its notes, disabled its weapons and surrendered, and was eventually shipped off planet. Marines Rider and Johnson were killed, and Cyber Knight was missing after packing himself away and slipping out of the base.

Calvin's conclusions are uncomfortable reading. The orders were carried out and the resistance neutralised, but it clearly had wide support, the Commnet was compromised, and the UW representative had ordered the execution of a UW citizen without charge or trial. He backed Oslo's question too: what is the legal position of troops who carry out orders they know to be illegal? The orders for [Mission 'Pegasus'](/mission/2494-06_mission-pegasus) 2 months later lacked an issuing authority as well. A year on, 5 Coy went back to Hal on [Mission 'Omaha'](/mission/2495-07_mission-omaha), this time on the rebels' side, and Calvin was glad of the chance to make up for the surrender. Cyber Knight turned up there too, and [In the Line of Fire](/article/in-the-line-of-fire/itlof_2496-05) later explained his disappearance: he'd got upset, packed himself into a storage crate, and been forgotten when the base was evacuated.
