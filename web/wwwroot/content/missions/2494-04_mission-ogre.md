The orders came over the CMC Commnet with no issuing officer, no Commnet ID and no document reference, which Officer Cadet Calvin's [report](/article/mission-reports/2494-04_mission-ogre) points out the system shouldn't even have accepted. The orders for [Mission 'Pegasus'](/mission/2494-06_mission-pegasus) would arrive the same way. In April 2494 Major Harris took 5 Coy to Hal, a slave-owning world outside the UW, posing as a reserve training unit of the Hal army. Their local contact, known only as "the Senator", had full authority to give them orders. The deal was that they would help him quietly crush a rebellion, and he would bring Hal closer to the UW and the Projectile Weapons Ban.

It soon became clear what kind of man they were working for. Every prisoner the Marines handed over was executed, including a captured rebel who kept insisting the Senator was a traitor. His own mole in the rebel ranks was tortured to death with a blade while his guards held the Marines back. Then Interstella's Krystal Spyers walked up with a squad of Hal regulars and picked out Harris by name. The UW representative arrived, admitted the real plan was to make the Senator president, and ordered Spyers executed. Harris refused to act without a signed written order. He got one, with no charges on it, took her outside, and she came back pronounced dead. The company still knocked out a Hal comms relay and hit the rebel leadership meeting, but on the Sunday a battalion of Hal regulars surrounded the base, addressed the Marines as "CMC 5 Coy" and gave the Senator 24 hours to leave the planet. Nobody fancied fighting on his behalf. 5 Coy destroyed its notes, disabled its weapons and surrendered. Marines Rider and Johnson were dead, and Cyber Knight had packed himself away and vanished.

Calvin's conclusions make uncomfortable reading. The orders had been carried out, but the rebellion clearly had wide support, the Commnet was compromised, and a UW representative had ordered the execution of a UW citizen without charge or trial. He backed Oslo's question too: what is the legal position of troops who carry out orders they know to be illegal? A year later 5 Coy went back to Hal on [Mission 'Omaha'](/mission/2495-07_mission-omaha), this time on the rebels' side, and found Cyber Knight still there. [In the Line of Fire](/article/in-the-line-of-fire/itlof_2496-05) later explained that he'd got upset, packed himself into a storage crate, and been forgotten when the base was evacuated.
