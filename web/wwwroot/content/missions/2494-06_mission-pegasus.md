Two years after a UW survey team went to X244 in the Sirent Cing system to investigate claims of alien artifacts, everyone sent after them had vanished too. That meant a 3 Coy rescue team, a UW threat response team and 4 private ships that had ignored the quarantine beacons. In June 2494 Major Harris took 5 Coy in with a UW Survey team under Dr Jones, an Intel-Corp executive called Mr Finch, and orders that, like those for [Mission 'Ogre'](/mission/2494-04_mission-ogre), had no issuing authority. Before the drop, Harris told his officers about his personal search for Surgeon Lt Tane Diamond, once of 5 Coy, and asked them to carry it on if he died.

The shuttle blacked out on approach and only just got down, and nothing on the ground matched the maps. A survey flight crashed 500m from base, killing Marine Reld and 3 of the scientists, and something dragged Dr Locket off into the woods. That something was the local scavenger: a stretched-out cat 4-5m long that could leap 8-10m and went for anything lying still. On a planet full of IR fire, that meant downed Marines. Striker and Adder were taken, and Finch was found mauled. A patrol found an abandoned camp with makeshift graves, a Brotherhood scarf, a sign reading "Welcome to the Slaughterhouse", and half-burned papers naming ex-Colonel Nasco. A family of castaways then convinced Harris they had landed on the wrong planet: X243, home of a secret CMC testing station. With Brotherhood guerrillas attacking throughout, the company traded with castaways for scraps of radio. On the second day they stormed a Brotherhood camp and freed the station's last 4 survivors, tied to trees, and one of them was Lt Diamond. A final raid produced the signal booster that let Calvin call the Garavaglia.

Calvin's [report](/article/mission-reports/2494-06_mission-pegasus) admits the mission raised more questions than it answered: other castaways, the X244 artifacts, and what X243 had really been for. A captured mercenary said the Brotherhood had been ordered to kill Diamond and anyone with her, and Harris reckoned Nasco had hired them to clean up. The station's memos had been in the briefing pack for [Mission 'Procyon'](/mission/2492-10_mission-procyon) 2 years earlier. Krystal Spyers, grateful to Harris for her life after Ogre, had passed him information on Diamond, which Calvin took as one more sign of a leak in CMC security. The months of quarantine that followed were still affecting the company on [Mission 'Nightside'](/mission/2495-01_mission-nightside), and this report was in the briefing pack for [Mission 'Red Storm'](/mission/2496-03_mission-redstorm), the mission that finally ended Nasco.
