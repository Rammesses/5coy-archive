Special Forces again, but this time the trail went back half a century. A raid on an SF regional base had turned up Project Gemstone: CMC Special Forces, the Restryk Corporation and a lot of UW money, all pointing at a rim world called Homeworld. Calvin asked Sgt Turner to [dig through IRAG](/article/in-character/2496-02_mission-intruder_comms-002), and the codewords came back with a "White" security rating, a classification nobody had heard of. Only a few months after the renegade SF on [Mission 'Nova' - November 2495 (Liahn)](/mission/2495-11_mission-nova), Captain Calvin took 5 Coy down in February 2496, with Acting 2nd Lt Turner as his second and a batch of recruits still to train. The locals were Amish, horrified by technology and by female Marines. They were very keen to tell the company that the people it had come to evacuate were all dead.

They weren't. A local called Luke came at the base in a berserk rage and kept going under IR fire even after his screen failed. When he came round he was calm and apologetic, and looking for his kidnapped daughter. These were the GEMs, Genetically Engineered Marines. Special Forces and Restryk had built them to resist IR, but they were passive until they snapped into uncontrollable rages, and when the project was shut down they were quietly dumped on Homeworld. Now Restryk teams were snatching children, Banodyne were politely offering contracts, and Puritan hit teams had already killed most of the GEMs. Calvin's attempt to fly Luke and 2 GEMs out ended with them setting on him and the dropship being shot down. What happened during Turner's rescue patrol led to a [Board of Inquiry](/article/mission-reports/2496-02_mission-intruder_sibreport) on the spot: Sgt Dibbley had cut a GEM's throat, Marine Klay Togin had beheaded one, and Marine Fugazi had delivered a coup de grace. Then Lt Col Svjenson delivered new UW orders: evacuate the GEMs or eliminate them. Sgt Mexal put down her weapon and refused, and Sgt Martins slipped away from his post, came back with her, and was arrested for desertion. Captain Bryant, leading the last GEMs as they broke out to go underground, died from the double dose of diazamorphite it took to hold him. With its medical supplies exhausted, the company was declared unfit for duty and withdrawn.

Calvin's [report](/article/mission-reports/2496-02_mission-intruder) is blunt about it. GEMs resistant to IR could wreck the Projectile Weapons Treaty, the survivors were hiding with an old SF weapons cache, and the Amish were ready for holy war. His recommendation was to let "the surviving Marines live out their retirement unmolested". The [scenario](/article/scenarios/mission-intruder) had written the kill order in as its ending. In the event, CMC High Command [refused the order as illegal](/article/out-of-character/interim-history_2496-to-2501), and the "GEMS Incident" became a public inquiry that would follow 5 Coy for years.
