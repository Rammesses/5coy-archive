Special Forces again, but this time the trail went back half a century. A raid on an SF regional base had turned up Project Gemstone: CMC Special Forces, the Restryk Corporation and a lot of UW money, all pointing at a rim world called Homeworld. Calvin asked Sgt Turner to [dig through IRAG](/article/in-character/2496-02_mission-intruder_comms-002), and the codewords came back with a "White" security rating, a classification nobody had heard of. Only a few months after the renegade SF on [Mission 'Nova'](/mission/2495-11_mission-nova), Captain Calvin took 5 Coy down in February 2496, with Acting 2nd Lt Turner as his second and a batch of recruits still to train. The official job was to re-establish contact. The locals were Amish: polite, devout, and horrified by technology and by female Marines. They were very keen to tell the company that the people it had come to evacuate were all dead, and that it had 14 hours before its presence defiled the planet.

They weren't dead. A local called Luke came at the base in a berserk rage. He kept going under IR fire even after his screen failed, and was only stopped when he was hamstrung. When he came round he was calm and apologetic, and looking for his kidnapped daughter. Later he killed the man who'd betrayed her, with 4 Marines trying to hold him back. A beaten civilian called James, who looked barely 20, gave his year of birth as 2427. These were the GEMs, Genetically Engineered Marines. Special Forces and Restryk had built them to resist IR, but they were passive until they snapped into uncontrollable rages, and when the project was shut down they were quietly dumped on Homeworld. Now everyone wanted them. Restryk security teams were snatching children, Banodyne were politely offering contracts, and Puritan hit teams inside both corporations had already killed most of the GEMs. Amish zealots attacked behind an apparently bulletproof priest, and 2 news crews had hacked the UW library to get there first. CNN were thrown out of the base minus their film, and invoiced for the screen they were lent.

Calvin tried to fly Luke and 2 GEMs out, but they realised they'd been tricked and set on him, and the dropship was shot down. Turner led the rescue and was med-evaced with serious throat injuries. What happened on the way back led to a [Board of Inquiry](/article/mission-reports/2496-02_mission-intruder_sibreport) on the spot. Sgt Dibbley had cut a GEM's throat, Marine Klay Togin had beheaded one and started on another, and Marine Fugazi had delivered a coup de grace. Lt Rumpole recommended reprimands for 2 of them and a second-degree murder charge for Fugazi. On Day 2, Lt Col Svjenson delivered new UW orders: evacuate the GEMs, or if that wasn't possible, eliminate them along with anyone trying to take them. Sgt Mexal put down her weapon and refused. Sgt Martins slipped away from his post, came back with her, and was arrested for desertion. When the last GEMs, led by Captain Bryant, worked out what the orders meant, they broke out to go underground. Bryant died from the double dose of diazamorphite it took to hold him. With medical supplies exhausted, the company was declared unfit for duty and withdrawn.

Calvin's [report](/article/mission-reports/2496-02_mission-intruder) is blunt about it. GEMs resistant to IR could wreck the Projectile Weapons Treaty, the survivors were hiding with an old SF weapons cache, and the Amish were ready for holy war. His recommendation was to let "the surviving Marines live out their retirement unmolested". The [scenario](/article/scenarios/mission-intruder) had written the kill order in as its ending. In the event, CMC High Command [refused the order as illegal](/article/out-of-character/interim-history_2496-to-2501), and the "GEMS Incident" became a public inquiry that would follow 5 Coy for years.
