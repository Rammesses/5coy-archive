This time the enemy was the CMC's own. The UW Rapid Reaction Battalion had done so well that Special Forces were starting to look surplus, and the UW decided to disband them. Some SF units weren't going quietly. On Liahn, a third-generation colony out on the rim, they were running a "prolonged survival exercise". In reality they were leaning on the planetary council to break away from the UW, and they'd invited a few press agencies along so it would all look legitimate. In November 2495, Acting Captain Calvin took 18 Marines in to find them and arrest or kill them, as quietly as possible. Even getting there was in character: Capt Martins' [shuttle clearance](/article/in-character/2496-02_mission-nova_comms-001) for Sgt Turner reads remarkably like directions off the M4 at junction 6.

It started badly, with Dropship 1 landing straight into an SF ambush, and got murkier from there. A Military Intelligence "Agent McDonnald" arrived full of useful-sounding intel and led Cpl Beck's recce into an ambush. MFC Deacon turned up just afterwards to warn that it was a set-up, and McDonnald was found tied to a tree, shot. CNN arrived wanting to interview the survivors of the [Boarding Action - Marie Celesta - May 2495](/mission/2495-05_boarding-action-mariecelesta). Calvin turned their aggressive questions round by giving them an "exclusive as it happens" broadcast that exposed the whole SF plan to the council and the colonists. That persuaded Councillor Domminic to call an emergency council meeting, and he vanished on his way home. The squad tracked him by the implant every original colonist carries and got him back after a long firefight, but MFC Angel wasn't revived in time. Domminic's granddaughter Sylvia had slipped out of the base to look for him, and the next day he walked in carrying her body. It ended with a gunship dogfight overhead and an SF cargo shuttle caught on the ground, loaded with Bodonite ore.

Calvin's [report](/article/mission-reports/2495-11_mission-nova) admits it was "not the most tidy" operation. About 30 SF were accounted for, and with hindsight Sylvia's death might have been avoidable. What worried him most was unemployed ex-SF going to work for corporations and fanatics, and Special Forces' past caught up with 5 Coy only 3 months later on [Mission 'Intruder' - February 2496 (Homeworld / Main)](/mission/2496-02_mission-intruder). [In the Line of Fire](/article/in-the-line-of-fire/itlof_2495-12) traced the Bodonite to a batch stolen from Pholax 3, allegedly by 5 Coy's own Special Forces Master Sgt Gibson. The Liane government awarded Calvin its [Merit Star](/article/out-of-character/honours_1995-08-23) on behalf of everyone involved. And Deacon? The [scenario](/article/scenarios/mission-nova) makes the helpful second informant a fake who's after the Bodonite himself.
