This time the enemy was the CMC's own. The UW Rapid Reaction Battalion had done so well that Special Forces were starting to look surplus, and the UW decided to disband them. Some SF units weren't going quietly. On Liahn, a third-generation colony out on the rim, they were running a "prolonged survival exercise". In reality they were leaning on the planetary council to break away from the UW, and they'd invited a few press agencies along so it would all look legitimate. In November 2495, Acting Captain Calvin took 18 Marines in to find them and arrest or kill them, as quietly as possible. As far as the locals knew, 5 Coy were just the opposing force for the exercise. Even getting there was in character: Capt Martins' [shuttle clearance](/article/in-character/2496-02_mission-nova_comms-001) for Sgt Turner reads remarkably like directions off the M4 at junction 6.

Day 1 started badly. Dropship 1 landed straight into an SF ambush, and by the time Dropship 2's counter-attack arrived, everyone from the first drop except Marine Doe was down. The squad was harried all the way to the CMC base, which SF had clearly been using, since they had the same entry codes. A Military Intelligence "Agent McDonnald" arrived with a lot of useful-sounding intel. Then CNN arrived wanting to interview the survivors of the [Marie Celesta](/mission/2495-05_boarding-action-mariecelesta), with questions Calvin called aggressive and insensitive. Calvin turned that round by giving CNN an "exclusive as it happens" broadcast that exposed the whole SF plan to the council and the colonists. McDonnald then led Cpl Beck's recce straight into an ambush. MFC Deacon turned up just after they'd left to confirm it was a set-up, and the rescue team found McDonnald tied to a tree, shot. For good measure, the DEA raided the base on a tip-off and found a stash of ampoules by the medical store.

Councillor Domminic was convinced by the broadcast and called an emergency council meeting, then vanished on his way home. His granddaughter Sylvia came looking for him and slipped out of the base during dinner. Every original colonist carries an implanted tracker, so the squad followed his weak signal to a remote position and got him back after a long firefight. MFC Angel wasn't revived in time, and his body wasn't where he'd been seen to go down. Day 2 was no kinder. Sgt Turner's patrol was ambushed and had to be swapped for a prisoner, and Deacon was pulled out after an SF unit tried to execute him outside the base. At midday Domminic walked in carrying Sylvia's body. Then a second MI operative, codename SCRATCH, checked out with the G2. After a dogfight between gunships, the squad caught an SF cargo shuttle on the ground loaded with Bodonite ore. Later they brought down the SF gunship, and one of the prisoners pulled from the wreck was the man who'd cut off Recruit Bear's fingers.

Calvin's [report](/article/mission-reports/2495-11_mission-nova) admits it was "not the most tidy" operation. About 30 SF were accounted for, and Sylvia's death might, with hindsight, have been avoidable. What worried him most was a lot of unemployed ex-SF going to work for corporations and fanatics. He was right to worry about Special Forces, though not in the way he expected: their past caught up with 5 Coy only 3 months later on [Mission 'Intruder'](/mission/2496-02_mission-intruder). [In the Line of Fire](/article/in-the-line-of-fire/itlof_2495-12) found that the Bodonite came from the same batch stolen from Pholax 3, allegedly by 5 Coy's own Special Forces Master Sgt Gibson, who'd been missing since 2494. The [year-end round-up](/article/in-the-line-of-fire/itlof_2496-01) congratulated V Coy on filling in when Special Forces were pulled out of the field, and the Liane government awarded Calvin its [Merit Star](/article/out-of-character/honours_1995-08-23) on behalf of everyone involved. And Deacon? The [scenario](/article/scenarios/mission-nova) makes the helpful second informant a fake who's after the Bodonite himself, and Calvin had already listed him as a "suspect contact".
