Mission 'Procyon' is the earliest mission in the archive, and almost nothing of it survives beyond the [intelligence briefing](/article/mission-reports/2492-10_mission-procyon-intel). In September 2492, CSM Turner, attached to CHQ, sent Capt Gillian aboard the Garavaglia II the database extracts for a contact mission to Laverii 42-6. The orders were to follow standard contact procedure, with medical isolation and linguistic analysis, and to show "all courtesy" to the Xeno Advisor going with them. There was also a warning of possible corporate interest in the artifacts. The restricted data on Laverii 42-6 itself was locked to voiceprint only, and there was nothing at all on the planet, the survey team or what they'd recovered. Turner's addendum read simply: "Be careful out there..."

The rest of the pack was stranger. It held the transmitter log of a CMC testing station on X243 and a trail of classified memos to Colonel Nasco at Area Command. Surgeon Lt Diamond had written to General Sullivan about the station's alarming accident rate, after the station commander ordered her to stop wasting CMC time. The next memo reported her death in a prototype exo-suit, with the body carried off by the local wildlife before anyone could reach it. The last one reported a reactor explosion, 11 dead or missing, and a cryptic "Suspect birds discovered cage".

No report of the mission itself survives, so how 5 Coy got on at Laverii 42-6 is anyone's guess. The X243 papers didn't go away, though. On [Mission 'Pegasus'](/mission/2494-06_mission-pegasus) in June 2494 the company crash-landed on X243, found the same memos in the ruins of the station, and found Lt Diamond alive.
