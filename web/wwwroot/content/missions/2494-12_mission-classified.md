Officially, this one was a training exercise. Lt Calvin's [report](/article/mission-reports/2494-12_mission-classified), stamped C L A S S I F I E D, describes a live-firing search-and-rescue exercise against unnamed elements of Special Forces playing irregulars. The objectives were to recover a downed dropship's flight recorder and crew, keep contact with the locals to a minimum and sanitise the crash site, with exercise referees on hand to treat the "casualties". [Mexal's letter home](/article/mexals-letters/mexals-letters_2494-12_juliette), dated 5 December 2494, tells a different story.

The Garavaglia had dropped out of jump near Juliette, a militarised dictatorship outside the UW. A covert UW dropship had crashed there with Capt Zwalf aboard, and if the Juliette government found evidence of it, it could mean war. 5 Coy went down carrying sidearms only and no IDs, posing as Juliette's 3rd Army hunting a "meteorite". Their orders came from the Supreme Commander himself: terminate with extreme prejudice anyone who saw through them. Fugazi stood up and said what everyone was thinking. In fog and freezing rain they found their contact in the hands of the Special Police, and the codeword "Mario" dealt with the police. Scavengers who'd dived the reservoir turned up with the flight recorder and a briefcase of papers, then got their friends back in a raid on the unscreened safe house, which Calvin had torched as the company left. At the dam, Zeus and Cassell were taken hostage in exchange for the briefcase, and Turner and Mexal emptied it before handing it over. The site warden grew suspicious and was killed, and later his wife came asking after him. Zwalf was found being led away by the dam guards. The orders said to leave anyone who couldn't walk, but the company carried its wounded onto the dropships anyway.

Back aboard, there was a Regimental Dinner, a pleased High Command, and the message that "this mission didn't happen". It kept on not happening. At the briefing for [Mission 'Nightside'](/mission/2495-01_mission-nightside), Major Harris held a conversation about Marines overstepping the mark that officially "has not taken place", and Martins was demoted soon afterwards. By the [Marie Celesta](/mission/2495-05_boarding-action-mariecelesta) there were accusations of torturing prisoners and a Juliette TV drama about the affair, and Mexal heard on [Mission 'Omaha'](/mission/2495-07_mission-omaha) that it was still causing diplomatic turmoil. She also noticed that the briefcase held 5 ID cards, none of them Zwalf's, from a dropship that supposedly carried only 4 people.
