Grant's World was meant to be a quiet one. A marginal Class M planet, it was being made ready by a UW-funded colony prep team as a new home for Groombridge refugees. Then their equipment started to disappear, and then their people, until half the team were dead or missing. The Colonial Survey's investigators blamed the colonists and left, and their shuttle vanished from the Lone Star's sensors with a last transmission of "My God what's that... the light, the light". In January 2495 Major Harris took 5 Coy down under cover of a search-and-rescue training mission.

Grant's World was odd from the start. There were patches of white fog in bright sunshine, a masked figure in black who couldn't be caught, and rings of white powder. Analysis showed the powder was metal, plastic and animal tissue reduced to their component molecules: all that was left of the shuttle and its passengers. The hostiles were Hengists, a cult convinced that friendly aliens were coming, who wanted the colonists cleared out first. They were as baffled by the disappearances as anyone. Two black androids, the Soldier and the Jester, came and went with a whistle and a bang. Farquhar dropped in to offer the Marines a free ticket off the planet, then walked away from the base and vanished in a red flash and a circle of white dust. A bomb turned up in the base, and proved to be practically empty once Cardinal and Mexal had blown the detonator off it. Then a missing colonist staggered back in, babbling about "men with stars in their eyes" and "the big pink". The trail led to a gully where Marines Wellesley and Riccotonini lay inside another circle. Riccotonini was dragged out and Capt Zwalf charged in for Wellesley. There was a whistle, a flash and a bang, and both of them were gone.

Calvin's [report](/article/mission-reports/2495-01_mission-nightside) concluded that someone was using teleportation, or faking it, and warned it might be a stunt like the Boeing-McDonnell Douglas JSE hoax of 2183. [Mexal's letter](/article/mexals-letters/mexals-letters_2495-01_grants-world) suspected a working matter transporter, with the Hengists as gullible cover. Calvin's guess that "we may yet see Capt. Zwalf and Marine Welesley again" turned out right. Zwalf was back in command for [Mission 'Achilles' - April 2495 (Groombridge)](/mission/2495-04_mission-unknown), though far from his right mind, and Wellesley had returned by the time of the [Boarding Action - Marie Celesta - May 2495](/mission/2495-05_boarding-action-mariecelesta). Farquhar didn't stay gone either, and turned up again on [Mission 'Red Storm' - March 2496 (Sirius)](/mission/2496-03_mission-redstorm). The [honours list](/article/out-of-character/honours_1995-08-23) decorated Beck and Cardinal for removing the bomb, and Zwalf for going into the circle after Wellesley.
