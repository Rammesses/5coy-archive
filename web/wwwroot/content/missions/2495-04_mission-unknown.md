The last time 5 Coy went to Groombridge, on [Mission 'Vortex' - November 2493 (Groombridge)](/mission/2493-11_mission-vortex) in November 2493, the civil war ended with a terrorist nuclear device and massive geological damage, and most of the planet had since been evacuated. Now Professor Pruwitt had a UW-backed plan to release the pressure building up in the planet's core by setting off more nuclear charges at carefully chosen sites. In April 2495, 5 Coy were sent to take over from 1 Coy guarding his team. Capt Zwalf was in command, back from [Mission 'Nightside' - January 2495 (Grant's World)](/mission/2495-01_mission-nightside). The few locals left and the environmentalists objected to more nuclear blasts, usually violently. The weekend even began with a Board of Inquiry into Marine Fugazi, who had stabbed a witch on Connet with salt-coated knives.

On the ground there was no building screen and radiation everywhere. On the first patrol 1 Coy's men attacked civilians on sight, and Zwalf, plainly not himself since Grant's World, tried to strangle their sergeant and then MFC Zeus. Sgt Chaos relieved him of command, and Major Harris came down to take over. The environmentalists harried the squads planting Pruwitt's detonation probes. They stripped Sgt Turner's squad of drugs, maps and radios, and left an incendiary on Cassell. The worst moment came at the base steps, where a badly contaminated girl called Jenna begged the Marines to kill her and then bolted for the door. Mexal grabbed her to keep her out, and O'Shaunessy saw no other way than to have Martins shoot Mexal from behind and put her into isolation. Jenna, unscreened, was left to die. Then a technician vanished with one of the 2 main charges, and was tracked down with a group of mercenaries rumoured to be Xeno Corps Security. Nobody could safely carry the charges for more than 10 minutes, so Burke and Rocktansky, both Groombridge natives, volunteered to plant them.

Decontamination brought the real cost. Major Harris, Sgt Chaos, MFC Zeus and Marines Cardinal, Miller and Darling had absorbed fatal doses and had only days to live, and they chose to stay on the surface to monitor the detonations. High Command promoted them all, and the [honours list](/article/out-of-character/honours_1995-08-23) recognised them "for making the supreme sacrifice on behalf of the population of Groombridge". Mexal spent weeks in the isolab, and she was still tiring easily on [Mission 'Omaha' - July 2495 (Hal)](/mission/2495-07_mission-omaha). The intercepted signal filed with [her letter](/article/mexals-letters/mexals-letters_2495-04_groombridge) hints at who else was watching: someone had paid "Stoppers" to wreck the plan. Their paymasters were the same corporate correspondents who'd be fretting about the [Boarding Action - Marie Celesta - May 2495](/mission/2495-05_boarding-action-mariecelesta) a month later.
