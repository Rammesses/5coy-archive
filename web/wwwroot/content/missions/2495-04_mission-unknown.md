The last time 5 Coy went to Groombridge, on [Mission 'Vortex'](/mission/2493-11_mission-vortex) in November 2493, the civil war ended with a terrorist nuclear device and massive geological damage, and most of the planet had since been evacuated. Now Professor Pruwitt had a UW-backed plan to release the pressure building up in the planet's core by setting off more nuclear charges at carefully chosen sites. In April 2495, 5 Coy were sent to take over from 1 Coy guarding his team. Capt Zwalf was in command, back from [Grant's World](/mission/2495-01_mission-nightside). The few locals left and the environmentalists objected to more nuclear blasts, usually violently. Before the drop there was a Board of Inquiry on the Garavaglia into Marine Fugazi, who had stabbed a witch on Connet with salt-coated knives, and O'Shaunessy along with her. He swore his oath "so help me Arnie". The murder charge was dropped, but he was sent for psychiatric evaluation and a court martial for the assault.

On the ground there was no building screen, radiation hot-spots everywhere, and desamine for everyone. On the first tour, 1 Coy's men attacked some civilians on sight, and Zwalf, plainly not himself since Grant's World, tried to strangle their Sgt Walker and then went for MFC Zeus. Sgt Chaos relieved him of command with the Surgeon Commander's backing, and Zwalf had to be dragged off and locked up until Major Harris came down to take over. Pruwitt's technicians then had the squads planting spiked "detonation probes" against the clock. Sgt Turner's squad was ambushed at its site and stripped of drugs, maps and radios. The attackers dosed them with diazamorphite and left an incendiary on Cassell, which Mexal knocked off with a long stick. It looked like environmentalists who wanted to slow the Marines down rather than kill them.

The worst moment came at the base steps. A woman left a young girl called Jenna there and ran. Jenna was badly contaminated, with burns and strange blue-green staining on her skin, and she begged the Marines to kill her. When she bolted for the door, Mexal grabbed her to keep her out of the base, and O'Shaunessy saw no other way than to order Martins to shoot Mexal from behind and put her into isolation under diazamorphite. Jenna, unscreened, was left to die. Then one of Pruwitt's technicians vanished with one of the 2 main charges during an ambush in a building electrical storm, and the other team detonated theirs regardless. Next morning a coded message mentioning "Burke" gave the Garavaglia a grid reference, where the company found the renegade technician, the bomb and a group of heavily armed mercenaries rumoured to be Xeno Corps Security. Nobody could safely carry the charges for more than 10 minutes, so Burke and Rocktansky, both natives of Groombridge, volunteered to plant them.

Decontamination brought the real cost. Major Harris, Sgt Chaos, MFC Zeus and Marines Cardinal, Miller and Darling had absorbed fatal doses and had only days to live, and they chose to stay on the surface to monitor the detonations. High Command promoted them all, Harris to Lieutenant Colonel, and the [honours list](/article/out-of-character/honours_1995-08-23) recognised them "for making the supreme sacrifice on behalf of the population of Groombridge". Mexal spent weeks in the isolab, and she was still tiring easily on [Mission 'Omaha'](/mission/2495-07_mission-omaha). The intercepted signal filed with [her letter](/article/mexals-letters/mexals-letters_2495-04_groombridge) hints at who else was watching: someone had paid "Stoppers" to wreck the plan, and their corporate paymasters were worried about what a habitable Groombridge would do to their operations. They were the same correspondents who'd be fretting about the [Marie Celesta](/mission/2495-05_boarding-action-mariecelesta) a month later.
