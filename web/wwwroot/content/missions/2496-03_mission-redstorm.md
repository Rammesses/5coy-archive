The call for help came from Roger DeSignieur. Baron Lukel, the hereditary ruler of Sirius, had died in mysterious circumstances, and his brother wanted the CMC to "help control riots" while he took the throne, even though the succession belonged to the Baron's daughter Amy, away at university off-world. A second message, from one of the late Baron's staff, warned that something was wrong with the terraforming atmosphere processor. It was cut off at source, and DeSignieur insisted the sender was hysterical. In March 2496 Captain Calvin took 5 Coy down with a UW science adviser, Dr Floyd, onto a privately owned planet where 95% of the population were indentured servants. The orders were to support the science team, see the succession done properly, and expect DeSignieur to try and take over the company's chain of command. Acting 2nd Lt Turner wrote the [mission report](/article/mission-reports/2496-03_mission-redstorm), and the briefing pack pointed back to [Mission 'Pegasus'](/mission/2494-06_mission-pegasus) and years of Interstella coverage.

Day 1 set the tone. Two of the Baron's staff, Sara and John, were hiding in the base from DeSignieur's secret police, the "Night Hawks". Clifford J. Farquar, the CMC's favourite gun-for-hire, now DeSignieur's "security advisor", dropped off a set of atmospheric monitoring cards. Then a civilian choked to death outside the base with clear airways and no visible cause, and his card had changed colour. At DeSignieur's inspection his guards dragged Sara in, Mexal and Riccottannini broke ranks to protect her, and Farquar pulled a projectile weapon. Minutes later Alpha found her shot in the head in the treeline. Dr Floyd's diagnosis was worse still: an "oxygen-repellant" molecule that clumped at ground level, moved like mercury, ignored the wind and left no oxygen at all, so respirators were useless. If it was being made faster than it decayed, the whole atmosphere would go. A pipeline from a Garp Corporation plant through DeWitt's mining land to the processor looked like the culprit. And "Viper", the rebel leader Calvin went to meet that night, turned out to be ex-CMC Colonel Nasco, the man behind the attempted genocide on Altair-4. He took Calvin at gunpoint with a projectile weapon. The real Viper turned up at the base later, wondering why nobody had come.

Day 2 got worse before it got better. Farquar brought Nasco's terms: Calvin for Amy. Turner had to kneel unscreened in front of Nasco to hear them. A Garp representative then walked in with a cowed DeSignieur to announce that Garp and DeWitt had bought Sirius outright and the Marines should leave. The UW didn't recognise the sale. Amy herself turned up at the gate. An oxygen pocket drifted into the base, and Marine Woe, resting by the fire, died of anoxia before anyone found him. Then Viper reported that the corporations were fortifying the whole planet as a military base against the UW, and the Rules of Engagement became fire-first. Farquar could see Nasco would execute him once he stopped being useful, so he sold Nasco out for the dropping of a murder charge. Turner laid the ambush, but not quite where Farquar suggested. At the signal Calvin pulled a knife on Nasco. Nasco shot Calvin in the leg, shattered Sgt Martins' pelvis, and when the Marines took him down he turned the gun on himself. It jammed. Turner would happily have finished the job, but Lt DeLima insisted Nasco be brought in alive. Then Sgt Dibbley "mistakenly" gave him a fatal overdose, and the CASEVAC dropship landed on his body in a crosswind. Martins died of his wounds back on the G2.

[In the Line of Fire](/article/in-the-line-of-fire/itlof_2496-04) led with "NASCO DEAD!", noting that CMC HQ had recorded the cause of death as unknown. Martins had been due to go home to Kruger in July after 5 years with the company, and Dibbley transferred out soon afterwards, with rumours that it wasn't his choice. Turner's report blamed Garp and DeWitt for the Baron's death from the start and called for Sirius to be interdicted. Sure enough, Sirius [seceded from the UW](/article/out-of-character/interim-history_2496-to-2501) as the wholly owned property of the two corporations. For anyone keeping score, a D.E. Nasco had already turned up on the [Marie Celesta](/mission/2495-05_boarding-action-mariecelesta)'s passenger list, and the report also noted that Mexal would put her moral code above orders, as she had on [Mission 'Intruder'](/mission/2496-02_mission-intruder).
