The call for help came from Roger DeSignieur. Baron Lukel, the hereditary ruler of Sirius, had died in mysterious circumstances, and his brother wanted the CMC to "help control riots" while he took the throne. The succession actually belonged to the Baron's daughter Amy, who was away at university off-world. A second message, from the late Baron's staff, warned that something was wrong with the terraforming atmosphere processor, and was cut off at source. In March 2496 Captain Calvin took 5 Coy down with a UW science adviser, Dr Floyd, to a privately owned planet where 95% of the population were indentured servants. Acting 2nd Lt Turner wrote the [mission report](/article/mission-reports/2496-03_mission-redstorm), and its briefing pack pointed back to [Mission 'Pegasus'](/mission/2494-06_mission-pegasus).

Sirius was being poisoned and run by thugs. DeSignieur's secret police, the "Night Hawks", were wiping out the Baron's staff. At his inspection of the company, his guards dragged in Sara, one of the staff who'd been hiding in the base, and Clifford J. Farquar, now DeSignieur's "security advisor", pulled a projectile weapon. Minutes later she was found shot in the treeline. Dr Floyd traced a string of choking deaths to an "oxygen-repellant" molecule that clumped at ground level, moved like mercury and ignored the wind. It seemed to be leaking from a Garp Corporation pipeline to the processor. "Viper", the rebel leader Calvin went to meet, turned out to be ex-CMC Colonel Nasco, the man behind the attempted genocide on Altair-4, and he took Calvin at gunpoint. Then Garp and DeWitt announced they'd bought the planet outright, and an oxygen pocket drifted into the base and killed Marine Woe. Farquar was sure Nasco would execute him once he stopped being useful, so he sold Nasco out. Turner laid the ambush, and at the signal Calvin pulled a knife. Nasco shot Calvin and Sgt Martins, then turned his gun on himself, but it jammed. Lt DeLima insisted he be taken alive, Sgt Dibbley "mistakenly" overdosed him, and the CASEVAC dropship landed on his body in a crosswind. Martins died of his wounds back on the G2.

[In the Line of Fire](/article/in-the-line-of-fire/itlof_2496-04) led with "NASCO DEAD!", noting that HQ had recorded the cause of death as unknown. Dibbley transferred out soon afterwards, and rumour said it wasn't his choice. Turner's report blamed Garp and DeWitt for the Baron's death, and Sirius duly [seceded from the UW](/article/out-of-character/interim-history_2496-to-2501) as the property of the two corporations. For anyone keeping score, a D.E. Nasco had already turned up on the [Marie Celesta](/mission/2495-05_boarding-action-mariecelesta)'s passenger list. The report also noted that Mexal would put her moral code above orders, as she had on [Mission 'Intruder'](/mission/2496-02_mission-intruder).
