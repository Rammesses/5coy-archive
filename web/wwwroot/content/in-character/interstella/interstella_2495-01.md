### 5 Coy's Xmas Bash...

5 Coy recently has its regimental
dinner. As usual spirits were high, and
the company showed it hadn't forgotten
how to party. Some notable absences
included Cpl Silver, Major Harris and 5
Coy's own sex symbol - Marine Cybil.

Reliable sources assure us that Major
Harris was delayed at CMC HQ
attempting to quosh rumours of 5 Coy
being involved in the alledged UW
incusion onto the planet Juliet. Cpl
Silver was on call at Medcentre. Marine
Cybil was however less fortunate - she
was involved in a personal transporter
collision during her R & R after being
involved in the fiasco on Hal. The hand
injuries received during this accident
have almost healed but she did not
receive the very sudden recall to the
Caravaglia as she was not fully fit for
active duty. Although our sources
would not comment, it strikes me as
very odd that a marine would have to
be fit for active duty to attend a
regimental dinner!

Official announcements made at the
dinner included the promotion of Cpl
O'Shaunessy to Sergeant, and the
confirmation of Act Sgt Chaos as both
sergeant and officer trainee on the next
OTC course. Marines Zeus and Cassel
were promoted to Marine First Class
(MFC) - apparantly due to their heroic
actions in the field, again our source
would not divulge which field of battle
was being referred to. Marine Axe was
conformed as corporal, and provided
the toast from the lower ranks. The
ladies of 5 Coy were undoubtedly the
highlight of the evening and must be
some of the most attractive women in
the employ of the CMC.

Sgt Chaos was unwilling to comment
about earlier reports in this paper
linking him with Cpl Silver he
maintained they were "just good
friends". He was however seen
massaging Sgt O'Shaunessy's shoulders
fueling even more speculation - the
CMC's press controls prevent us
printing this picture at this time. Just to
confuse matters even more it was also
reported to me that Sgt Chaos had been
seen chatting up Cpl Hunter,
undoubtedly trying to secure a nice soft
landing!

The most unusual presentation of the
evening was bestowed upon Mst Sgt
Kramer who was knighted by HRH
Prince Frogassi of the Free State of
Avelonia, who is currently a serving
marine with 5 Coy.

Whilst talking to the members of 5 Coy
I was keen to find out if the rumours of
their involvement on Juliet were true. I
was unable to obtain any comment,
although the alledged slaughter of
innocent people does sound very much
like 5 Coy's "modus operandi". The
code of silence obviously meant
something but 5 Coy would obviously
deny any involvement whether it was
true or not.

Whilst having the opportunity to talk to
various members of 5 Coy whilst under
the influence I also tried to ascertain
comments about the alledged racism in
the unit. Again the code of silence
meant information was slow in coming
although mutterings of "pointy eared
goits" and "ruddy spagetti eaters" led
me to believe that there is definitely
something more to these allegations...

Happy New Year! Igot Thadirt.
