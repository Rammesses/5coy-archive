### Refugees win Court battle
_Filed by: Krystal Spyers - Heurls, Feb 2495_

Following 5 Coy's findings on their
mission to Heurls last month, the UW
Court has found in favour of a group of
Groombridge refugees, who were
proven to be the legal owners of the
disputed land.

The dispute arose over a seam of
valuable Dracken ore discovered on
land near Sheeple. Iowa Power and De
Witt Mining both unwittingly purchased
'fake' claims from two prospectors
(Ivan Bilk and Renny Vark), and both
believed the territory to be their own.

The town of Sheeple claimed that the
mineral rights to the land were theirs,
conveniently forgetting that they had
already been paid by the UW for the
land, which was to be given to the
refugees - a fact which the Councillors
concerned had kept from the townsfolk.

Prospectors from across the galaxies
descended on the site, all eager for a
chance to discover a trace of Draken.
However, they were all expelled from
the area by 5 ,Coy who were clearing
the area pending the Court's ruling.
One prospector later complained that he
had been shot at by the marines, who
had to administer anti-IR drugs.

An Iowa Power security team
dispatched to the area committed
several illegal acts, including murder,
for which they attempted to frame De
Witt operatives. The deception was
uncovered by 5 Coy during the course
of their duties.

There was also trouble from many local
youths who had an axe to grind about
the influx of refugees into their area.
They were supplied with guns and
screens, and paid an undiclosed sum to
drive the refugees from the disputed
territory. Their source has not yet been
identified. Two of them - Simon (20)
and Julie Gavin (15) - were arrested by
5 Coy following an attack on the
refugee camp. They have since said
that apart from being beaten around the
head and not being fed for 24 hours
they were treated reasonably fairly.

However, Julie - legally classified as a
minor - has since been diagnosed as
suffering from concussion, alledgedly
as a result of being beaten unconscious
by one of the marines.

Not all of 5 Coy's visitors were hostile
though - a local Eco-group arrived to
complain about the damage and
disruption to the ecology of the
disputed site, which is designated as an
area of outstanding beauty. After a
short but peaceful demonstration, the
group left.

The merc camp was finally overrun by
5 Coy, with only one known survivor.
However, this single merc went on to
capture the marine base single-handed,
before escaping with the marines' entire
drug supply. No trace of her has been
found.

Sgn Mjr Pandawawa is recovering after
being knifed in a frenzied 'revenge'
attack by a Groombridge woman, one
of the many who still hold 5 Coy
responsible for the tragedy that befell
their homeworld. She was successfully
treated for these injuries at the time, but
her wounds were later opened up when
she was attacked by the surviving merc.

Sheeple Councillors Morrison, Holly
and Giorgio have since been charged
with fraud and corruption. They have
admitted to initially hiring the
mercenary group for protection, but
stated that the mercs later ran out of
their control.

### Time Gate or Teleport?

Following 5 Coy's visit last January
into the 'Twilight Zone', it has emerged
that one of the Coy's officers believes
that the strange 'time gate' is in fact a
teleportation device.

His theory follows the strange
disappearance of Professor Thom
Merrilion whilst holidaying on Earth
last year. Merrilion allegedly sold a
prototype teleport device that he had
developed to an Exec for Malenski
Transportation.

Whether the device worked or not is
not known, but if so this could explain
the mysterious disappearance of various
priceless art treasures from the most
secure vaults of Groombridge in the
few months before tragedy struck!

If a working teleportation device has
been developed, security considerations
across the galaxies will have to be
completely reworked. Rumours have
already begun to circulate that the UW
President Vanessa Parnell recently
instigated research into a 'teleportation
screen' to prevent unauthorised
appearances in the middle of UW
Council sessions.

### Juliet prepares for war

The authorities on Juliet are close to
declaring war on the UW, following
'allegations' that 5 Coy were on a
shoot-to-kill covert mission there last
December.

2 Coy have had all leave cancelled, and
are on standby in neighbouring
systems. The UW is now considering
what measures it can - or wants to -
take to prevent Juliet attacks on UW
ships/planets.

### Hengists...

Senior members of the Hengist
movement have unreservedly
condemned the acts of violence and
civil disorder recently committed by its
so-called militant wing.

They have stated that any members
guilty of such disgraceful behaviour
will immediately have their membership
revoked, and will be banned from the
organisation for life.

They have offered the UW full access
to its membership 'list' to assist then
with their inquiries.

However, there is some basis for the
belief that the riots seen recently on
Dawn, Braconis and Heurls were the
work of outside agitators, assuming the
'role' of Hengists for the occaision.

### Refugees

Agitators are also thought to be behind
more rioting in various Groombridge
refugee camps in most of the
resettlement areas.

Many of the teenagers in the camps are
becoming more and more disillusioned
with the UW. They are becoming easy
prey for those who wish to stir up
hatred and bitterness to suit their own
ends.
