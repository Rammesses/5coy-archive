### Accident or Murder?
_Filed by: Krystal Spyers - CMC Med Centre, April 2495_

Last month 5 Coy were dispatched to Groombridge to
assist in a plan to stabilise the planet's core. Seven of
their number did not return. (The last time that 5 Coy
suffered such losses was at the hands of the League
Against Warfare (LAW) during the infamous St
Valentine's Day Massacre of 2492).

5 Coy is also temporarily without Capt Zwarf again, as
he would appear to have completely lost his mind just
after their landing. He assaulted a member of 1 Coy and
then attempted to strangle MFC Zeus. Needless to say
he was restrained, and together Sgn Cmdr
Mimiannapandawawa and Sgt Chaos declared him unfit
to command. Sgt Chaos took over until the arrival of
Major Harris.

I asked the Sgn Cmdr where Captain Zwarf was now,
and she told me that he had gone for medical
re-assessment. However, when questioned about the
actual level of his psychological damage she would
make no comment. Obviously I felt compelled to ask her
if she therefore considered that reports of him being a
raving homicidal maniac were completely unfounded -
"No comment" was the reply. I've found that you learn
more from what people DON'T say...

But Zwarf was lucky - he was frog-marched off-world
into a nice, cosy padded cell before he was in any
danger of accumulating a fatal dose of radiation. Seven
of his marines were not so lucky...

5 Coy were sent to an area more than well known for its
high radiation level. They were sent to escort and assist
in the placement of old and very dirty nukes that by all
accounts were leaking radiation even before they were
detonated. 26 marines were sent into these conditions
with 5 radiation suits. The marines who were fortunate
enough to wear those suits then refused to give them
up... but who could honestly say that they would not
have been tempted to do the same? The rest had no
protection whatsoever...

Yet the marines did their job. They carried out their duty
to the best of their abilities and beyond expectations, but
the reward for 7 of them was death... Not the relatively
painless death from an overload of I/R, but two days of
hell in which there was plenty of time to see in your
colleagues a mirror of your own degenerating condition.

All seven agreed to stay on planet and spend their final
hours helping to clean up the area and assisting any
:::

::: column-right
locals still in the area... Abandoned by their colleagues, and
berift of hope... Why weren't they rushed to Med facilities?
If the damage was irreverable why weren't they frozen until
technology could perform new miracles? Why were they left
on the planet that killed them as if they had done something
wrong - as if they were an embarassment?

Brewitt was an unreliable professor who used questionable
methods to achieve dubious aims. 5 Coy protected his labs
and escorted him and his techs on their duties, but how did
he repay them? By leading the marines straight through
several major hot zones, and giving conflicting and
erroneous details about the others. As for the result of his
explosions, initial results suggest that his bombs may indeed
have stabilised the core, but only temporarily - the eventual
destruction of the planet has only been delayed, not
prevented.

With the relatively ample resources of CMC HQ I find it
difficult to accept that they could only supply 5 suits to 5
Coy. Questions have to be asked - not just by me, but by the
marines themselves, and answers must be demanded.

Unfortunately there is a precedent for such a seemingly
deliberate attempt to 'do away with' a company of marines -
remember Altair IV? Remember Nasco?... One would almost
think that his hand was still at the helm...

The seven marines were all awarded the Gold Cross, and
were promoted in recognition for their bravery.

I knew most of the marines who died on that mission. I'd
crossed swords several times with Colonel Harris, who
despite his pig-headedness and arrogance obviously cared
very deeply for his people. Some of them have given or sent
me their last words to publish - some would give none lest
they be remembered for their resentment...

Sgt Cardinal told me that "I was unlucky - I wish we'd had
better kit - another crap mission for 5 coy - it's just shite isn't
it - it's a bum job..."

The deaths of more marines on Groombridge has opened the
floodgates for tributes, opinions and accusations. Some of
them follow:

Sgt Zeus brought much laughter to 5 Coy. She was like a
breath of fresh air, and her absence will be more than deeply
felt.

Sgt Miller was only on his second mission with 5 Coy. A
good marine who'd had a promising future.

Sgt Cardinal's explosives skills had saved 5 Coy on more
than one occaision. He was a well liked member of the
Corps.

Sgt Darling was on his first mission with 5 Coy. He was
known for his strong hand in a pressure situation -
something for which 1 Coy can testify.

Corporal Sabre - though only a recruit when he landed on
Groombridge - had already made his mark. He was a
young marine who was taken in his prime.

Lieutenant Chaos finally achieved that rank, despite a
deluge of bad paperwork. He was a man of good humour,
and will be missed by more than a few of 5 Coy's ladies.

Colonel Harris sometimes cared more about his marines
than his orders and objectives. He was a man of good
humour and loud voice. 5 Coy will not be the same without
him.

I also asked the Sgn Cmdr what she thought about the
deaths of so many marines. She said - it was a tragedy that
should never have happened, and a thorough investigation
should be launched".

I asked if anything could be done to save them. She
replied "the damage caused by the very high levels of
radiation cannot at this point be reversed or even slowed.
We need more funds for research... Leaving those people to
die on planet was really horrible. Just abandoning 6 people
that you've worked with... what a horrible way to die - you
can see your colleagues dying around you... and we just
left them there with our entire stock of tranqs and
analgesics..."
