### CMC's 5 Coy On Kruger Shock
_Filed by: Krystal Spyers - Kruger, Mar 2493_

A communique from a member of 5 Coy to her family has come into our
possession. This astonishing account of her experiences with 5 Coy
talks of secret satellites, nuclear bombs, cyborgs in the Corps, and
of a CMC mission on Kruger, supposedly a neutral world!

This marine, who in case of legal proceedings against her we shall
call Marine X for now, was a recruit in 5 Coy, undergoing her basic
training. She is from a well respected family, with connections to
the One True Church.

Her letter begins with general details of training, but soon hots up
with talk of a bit of bother the recruits had had with the Brotherhood
early on, and how they'd got out of difficulties by hightailing it
back to the safety of their base.

It was not long thereafter, writes Marine X, that Colonel McKiernan
(judging by the description given) contacted 5 Coy, regarding a
satellite that had crashed nearby. Sabotage was suspected - thus
explaining the Brotherhood's presence on Kruger.

**Military satellite**

According to Marine X, this satellite contained equipment of a highly
secret military nature, and also a nuclear self-destruct and
anti-tampering mechanism! The disarming sequence was decoded by 5 Coy,
who then set out to retrieve the satellite before the Brotherhood
captured it.

**15 seconds from nuclear hell**

The disarming code was stated in an uncoded form in the letter - again
for security reasons we are obviously not printing it here! According
to Marine X, the code was entered with just 15 seconds to go...

**CMC Cyborg**

On returning to their base, 5 Coy lost their commanding officer (which
is getting to be a bit of a habit). Another marine was listed MIA, but
his body was returned to 5 Coy the following day. Marine Cyberknight
was seriously injured as he unsuccessfully attempted to defuse the
booby-trapped body, and here Marine X reveals that he was actually a
cyborg! Whilst the existence of cybernetically enhanced personnel is
not illegal, many people would not feel happy about a cyborg on the
loose, especially with 5 Coy. This is likely to further enrage the
Puritans, who already decry the use of clones in the Corps.

5 Coy's commanding officer was finally rescued by the marines after
another fierce battle with the Brotherhood, and the satellite was
safely returned.

I contacted the Kruger authorities about the presence of marines and
a nuclear device on their world. They were shocked at the revelations,
and said that they will be demanding a full explanation from both the
Corps and the UW, and that depending on the response legal proceedings
would commence.

The satellite in question was listed as a normal comms and weather
beacon, but bearing in mind its true nature, the thought of nuclear
devices circling above our heads certainly adds more than a little
spice to life!

I am sure the CMC will be holding its own inquiry into the disclosure
to non-marine personnel of such sensitive material, together with the
disarming sequence for a nuclear device!

Colonel McKiernan refused to comment when questioned earlier, but he
looked decidedly short of happy!

Whilst all this has made very interesting reading and I must thank
Marine X for her information, it does leave one feeling a little
unsure about the security of the Corps...

**FOOTNOTE:** Marine X also talked of 'elves' in her communique home.

Elves were mythical creatures back on good old Terra, along with
goblins, ogres and honest polititians.

They were supposedly a long-lived race, tall and slender, reputedly
very beautiful and had large, pointy ears - rather like the legendary
Star Trek's Mr Spock.

When experimentation into genetic enhancements began, scientists tried
to improve performance, eyesight, hearing and many other functions of
the human body. Much experimentation was halted when controlling
legislation was introduced, but the descendants of such altered
'stock' can sometimes still be seen today.

Those with pointed ears are among the most easily identified, and were
soon nick-named elves. All 'elves' originated from the Parnell labs on
Lumiere.

Perhaps the best known 'elf' was Sgn Lt Diamond, formerly of 5 Coy,
who is still missing following the evacuation of Altair IV in July
2491. CMC medical records include details of reconstructive surgery
performed on Diamond's ears, after the points were hacked off by the
Brotherhood on Golgotha.

The Puritans believe that elves join clones among the ranks of the
sub-human, and so it would seem that the persecution of elves
continues...

**NO END** was in sight to the Groombridge Civil War as talks broke
down between representatives of the Chidanian and Kasdunni
authorities. Limpz Schmidt the Kasdunni general walked out of the
talks announcing that they had nothing further to discuss.

Mella Beyo, who replaced William Ziathin the Chidanian leader murdered
in June last year, pledged that the Chidanians would fight to the last
man.

Both sides seem to have forgotten the reason why the conflict began,
and are now concentrating on fighting for fighting's sake. Outbreaks
of violence have occured across the planet, and there seems to be
little hope of preventing the fighting from escalating still further.

The UW have thrown a cordon around Groombridge, preventing the attacks
on ships that had threatened trade. The 'barricade' had been
supplemented by ships from Beta Hydri, whose people had expressed
grave concern of the proximity of the space battles to their world.

4 Coy have been assigned to the planet for the duration of the
conflict. Their casualties have mounted steadily since hostilities
began, as both sides regard them enemies.
