### Projectile Free-For-All On Fomalhaut

5 Coy would seem to be up to their old
tricks again, if CMC reports of their
recent visit to Fomalhaut are to be
believed! All in all, three projectile
weapons and a highly unstable frag
grenade were recovered from the base,
after extensive internal security
searches.

However, in addition to fighting such
internal corruption, 5 Coy were
responsible for a UW delivery of
radio-active materials to a new planet-
side Iowa Power site. Initially, the
commanding officer - Captain Gillham
was reluctant to hand over these
dangerous materials, due to what he
described as a "quite frankly ridiculous
lack of responsibility or intelligence"
on the part of the Iowa Power Security
Team, but finally the marines were
obliged to make the delivery.

According to later reports, Gillham's
fears were indeed founded, after a
terrorist attack was launched on the site
by the Brotherhood, using CMC-issue
weaponry! 5 Coy responded to an alert
broadcast by Captain Mickelhob from
the site, but was too late to save the
Iowa Power staff. The fissionable
materials were later recovered, but
leaky seals sparked off a radiation alert
in the midst of the CMC base.

During the course of their duties, two
members of 5 Coy died after attacks by
the Brotherhood. It is understood that
amongst the dead was Nick Stone, a
former lieutenant who was on trial for
charges under the Projectile Weapons
Act. It is perhaps ironic that Stone was
killed by associates of the very people
he murdered on Golgotha last year,
doubly so as if found guilty of the
above charges he was to have been
executed under recent UW legislation.

On a more amusing note, it was
announced last week that Domonic
Cantrel, sister to Dr Rachel Cantrel -
fiance of Sir(?) Torboran Haze - has
been drafted into 5 Coy, by order of his
father. Domonic apparantly passed his
basic training with flying colours, but it
is rumoured that his attitude still leaves
a lot to be desired. \*\* Unconfirmed
reports suggest that Domonic may have
been one of the marines contaminated
during the radiation alert. \*\*

As for the former Lt Stone, he who
lived by the sword, died by the sword...
