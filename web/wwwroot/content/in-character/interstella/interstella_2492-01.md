This month we are featuring the second
in our series of clone-rights articles. It
is a transcript of a speech given by
Angus McCall on Insulon III.

" Clones are as real as you and I. They
feel the same emotions that we do, they
have the same hopes and dreams, they
grow old and die, just as we their
human brothers do.

_...So why is it that they have no rights
whatsoever? They cannot vote, have
children, own possessions, or make
any sort of choice about their own
future._

Possibly the cruellest aspect of a
clone's life comes if they have been
raised by a human family as a normal
child. When they come of age they are
torn away from their home with no
warning whatsoever, and are then
stripped of their very identity. What
gives _us_ the right to treat them in this
way?

They can be beaten and even be
slaughtered like animals, but they have
no right of self defence, and the
perpetrators cannot be prosecuted.
There are laws to regulate the treatment
of livestock, _but there's nothing for our
brothers!_ They are legally regarded as
property - a commodity that can be
bought or sold, and disposed of without
the slightest thought.

They are lied to for most of their life,
and treated like shit for the rest of it.

Is this how we should treat our
brothers?...

_And we call ourselves humane..._ "

**UNITED WORLDS** - new cases
presented before the UW last month
include New Worlds bid to remove
legislation covering the terra-forming
of new worlds. Current laws force
terra-formers to prove world's to be
free of any sentient life-forms prior to
the commencement of operations. New
Worlds say that this is a completely
unnecessary precaution, as in all of the
worlds so far there has not been one
single sign of alien life.

The League Against Warfare (LAW) is
campaigning for the implementation of
more stringent checks on companies
and organisations to ensure that the
laws governing chemical and biological
warfare are being upheld. This follows
an explosion at a manufacturing com-
plex owned by the Erasmus Cor-
poration, following which many local
citizens had to receive treatment for a
variety of ills, ranging from extreme
nausea to temporary blindness. A spok-
esman for the Erasmus Corporation
stated that the chemicals released were
legal , and that as LAW themselves
had bombed the complex they were
responsible for any damages.
