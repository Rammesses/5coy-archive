### Ryan's World

Inter-tribal disputes have once again
erupted on the forbidden planet Ryan's
World.

For those who may not remember, this
backwater planet was 'forgotten' during
the Colonial War, and without support
from Old Earth its thriving civilisation
gradually degenerated into semi-
barbarism.

Old unstable space mines, together
with all sorts of junk and debris, had
created a virtual asteroid belt around the
area. Thus when a UW vessel
rediscovered the planet back in 2420,
they were unable to get through.
Neither were any answers received to
their signals, although after the
centuries it was extremely unlikely that
any comms equipment on planet was
operational, let alone have been tuned
into interstellar frequencies!

A Monitor-VIII satellite was left to
observe any life on the surface, and
soon a detailed record of life on Ryan's
World was compiled.

One of its most spectacular and
memorable transmissions came a year
later in 2421, and involved a renegade
vessel attempting to hide from a
pursuing CMC warship, amid the
'asteroid belt'. The pilot lost control,
and the ship was completely destroyed
as it crash landed on planet.

Socio-Historians have been monitoring
events on Ryan's World ever since, and
have just released the following update:

A band of locals from the village of
Bannockburn in the province of Reffley
formed a local militia to rout out a band
of mutants that had been terrorising the
area. Guided by the scav Harry, they
ran not only into the mutants, but also
into a mortar unit from the Columbian
Combo, a neighbouring country
supposedly adhering to a general truce.

However, whilst they were out on
patrol, Bannockburn was razed to the
ground by mortars, leaving them
homeless and their families dead...

Led by a grizzled campaigner Zack, the
villagers recaptured an old clan fort that
had been overrun by Combo troops.
They found files there, revealing that
one of the Combo's infamous
commanders was Colonel Nikita,
whose body was substantially
enhanced by cyber-implants. He was
however, suffering from severe neural
disorders, and was greatly feared by his
own troops.

One of the Combo's techs was found
to have been suffering from radiation
sickness, which was later traced to an
old, unstable nuclear device recovered
from an old weapons silo.

The trader who had been selling guns
to the mutants was finally tracked down
and killed by the young bounty hunter
Tau Shimmerman.

Though vastly outnumbered and out
gunned, the villagers held out against
the elite Dragoon troops, commanded
by their notorious colonel Nikita. Some
of the villagers - Zed, Randor and
Drake - devastated the Combo troops at
night, with Zed alone accounting for 19
sneak kills.

The dragoons armed the nuke when
their position was overrun by the
villagers, and it was finally disarmed by
the techs Charity and Reverand
Catherine, with 51 seconds to spare.

At first it was thought that Nikita had
been killed, but closer examination
proved that he'd used a body double
dressed in his uniform.

Two villagers died - Jac'T'We and Bob
Martins, but amongst the survivors
were Co-Co, Dane, Bill, Heggarty,
Halo Jones, Sparky, Sharra, Waylander,
Ashnak, Duncan MacLean, Joe
Pineapples and Don MacKenzie...

### Recruit Dies

A young recruit was killed last week,
two days before his nineteenth birthday,
whilst undergoing basic training with 2
Coy on Braconis.

Exact details of his death have not been
released, as the CMC are launching a
full scale inquiry, but it is thought to
have been accidental.

His name will not been released until
his relatives have been informed.

He is the second recruit to die this year,
and this has now prompted calls for
higher standards in the CMC's Basic
Training.

### IRAG

CMC HQ held a media conference
yesterday in which it admitted that the
Information Retrieval Advancement
Group's programme to supply marines
in the field with a much improved
information network had fallen behind
schedule and was over budget.

The head of IRAG, CSM Joel Turner,
blamed this reverse on a failure of both
Network Systems and Parnell to supply
equipment on time, causing the soft-
ware development time-scales to lag
accordingly.

He stated that initial trials of "Beta"
software would begin in the next few
months, following the installation of
hardware into CMC facilities by IRAG
staff.

CSM Turner has now been detached
from 5 Coy to formally head IRAG,
but said that he would be supervising
installation and trials himself, in the
field with 5 Coy, later this year.

### Flagship name mix-up

Embarassed CMC officals have just
discovered that 5 Coy's flagship, the
Caravaglia, has carried the wrong name
throughout its been in active service!

Signmakers apparantly misread the
spec, and misspelt the name Garavaglia
with a 'C'. The vessel was officially
launched by Vanessa Parnell, and
commentators at the time thought that
she had merely mispronounced the
vessel's name.

The CMC has declined to comment for
now, so it is not yet known whether the
ship will have the correct name-plate
attached, or whether it will continue to
be known as the Caravaglia...
