### CMC Guns Lost

Apparantly CMC HQ is in uproar today as rumours circulate that several
experimental squad heavy support weapons have gone missing from CMC
Research labs.

The CMC has not made any comment regarding the rumours, which are
believed to have originated from an employee at Alfatek, the company
who manufactures arms and equipment for the CMC and other security
forces.

Alfatek have also declined to comment.

### Yet More Thefts

A steady trickle of thefts of masterpieces, jewellery and objects of
historical value has continued to grow.

The robberies began in February when Winno's "Space Fatigue" was
stolen from the vaults of the First Bank of Groombridge.

No trace of forced entry has ever been found, and many are beginning
to believe that the PEC offial's quote of "vanishing into thin air"
may have some truth behind it. UW Investigators have been talking with
the boards of all the companies and corporations known to be
researching teleportation, but none are reported as being anywhere
near a breakthrough on the 'reassembly' problem.

Strange as it may seem, teleportation would seem to be the only way
that the items could have been removed from the vaults in which they
were secured. So as speculation runs rife in the halls of the
insurance companies, perhaps we should remember that truth is often
stranger than fiction...!

### Doctor Sentenced

Cassandra O'Shaunessy, the former doctor of 2 Coy was sentenced last
month to 17 years penal servitude, to run from the date the guilty
verdict was passed. O'Shaunessy was found guilty of malpractise and
membership of the League Against Warfare (LAW), and was dishonourably
discharged from the Corps.

Her time will probably be served at one of the infamous penal colonies
on the moons of the Altair system. Security is tight both on and off
the moons, and high levels of both soft (I/R) and hard (H/R) radiation
mean that it is not possible to survive outside the pressure domes,
even if they were breached.

O'Shaunessy has a twin sister Caitlain, who is a corporal in 5 Coy.
The CMC has stated that she is not available for comment.

### Kruger Outrage

Last April's report on illicit marine activities on the world of
Kruger have brought howls of outrage from the Krugerians, and the
incident seems set to be a major cause of embarassment for the CMC.

The story broke when a marine from 5 Coy 'wrote home' with full and
frank details of marine activities on the neutral world of Kruger.

According to her, its inhabitants were 15 seconds away from nuclear
devastation as 5 Coy clumsily tried to disarm the self-destruct
mechanism.

The Kruger Affair has sparked off huge rows in the UW, as other worlds
accuse the Krugerians of colaborating with CMC forces, and demand that
the planet's neutral status be removed.

### Parnell Sex Scandal

Sean Devlin Parnell, the 19 year old son of UW President Vanessa
Parnell, has been caught in bed with Jupiter Pinucchi, whose husband
Marcus is Managing Director of Blackwell Power.

Vanessa Parnell was unavailable for comment, but close friends have
hinted that she finds the whole affair amusing, and not in the least
dangerous to her career.

The lovers were discovered by a security consultant hired by Mr
Pinucchi to install security vids into the new annexe of his house. It
is not known whether the actions of the two were recorded for
posterity!

Mr Pinucchi has refused to comment on the affair due to the onset of
legal proceedings. It is understood that his wife has moved into the
Blackwell Power's suite in the Galactic Hilton.

**HENGISTS** from all over the galaxy are expected to be gathering on

Their application to visit the ancient Stonehenge site on Earth was

Subjects to be discussed include "When will the Aliens return to us
their Children" and "Religion - An Alien Concept?"

### Aunty Trisha...

This month I'd like to answer the following question, which is often
raised by readers.

_"My parents have signed me up into the Colonial Marine Corps, but I'm
not too sure. How can I get out of this?"_

Well, easier said than done, I'm afraid! Once signed up, you're in for
the duration - check your contract, but it's usually for a minimum
term of 15 years, with an option for the CMC to re-contract. You can't
leave unless it's feet first or you're dishonourably discharged.

And I wouldn't advise being a naughty boy/girl to try and gain a
discharge - just look what happened to O'Shaunessy, and also to
Warlock of 5 Coy, who was discharged just before his execution.

Just grin and bear it - you'll travel the universe and meet lots of
new people!
