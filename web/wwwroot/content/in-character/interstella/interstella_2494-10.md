### CMC flying high

Security Officers raiding an illegal rave
outside Parnell on Lumiere were
surprised to find 8 marines from 1 Coy
among the youngsters at the party. All
were charged with possession of illegal
hallucinagenic drugs. Such raves have
become popular with the youth of
Lumiere, who take a Boost derivative
to heighten their enjoyment of the
music and dancing.

The arrests are particularly embarassing
for the CMC as the marines involved
had been part of the honour guard
which had escorted Ehlana Parnell to
the spaceport two days previously. She
had spent a week with her family,
before the start of her much publicised
diplomatic mission to the Aldebaran
Union.

The incident has furthered calls at the
UW Senate for a fuller investigation of
the CMC by the UQDEA. "They must
learn that they are not above the Law"
said one senator. The debate continues.

### Groombridge

Rumours continue to fly regarding what
has been called a 'mad-cap' scheme to
save Groombridge.

The planet has been almost torn apart
since an old nuke was accidentally set
off above a sensitive fault line in
Chidan, triggering world-wide
earthquakes, tidal waves and volcanic
eruptions that have killed billions.

This latest scheme has emerged from
the team of scientists who have
dedicated the last 10 months of their
lives to Groombridge's plight. It is said
to involve the use of more nuclear
devices set at strategic points in the
planet's crust

But surely two wrongs don't make a
right...

publish her auto-biography.

This 'no holds barred' account of

There have been 4 more deaths from
toxin overload since our last report
back in July.

confirmed that the toxins were dumped
into their carriers bloodstreams by their
oral micro filters.

The manufacturer - who cannot be
named for legal reasons, but whose
daughter is affianced to the former
universal playboy once attached to 5
Coy - has denied any possibility that
his filters could be responsible.

Most of the filters were implanted in
colonists working in hazardous
enviroments, but several were
commisioned by individuals rich
enough or well connected enough to
afford them...

### MTC to be formed

CMC HQ revealed plans last month for
the Marine Training Corps. It will be
open to anyone in the 12 - 16 age
groups, regardless of gender.

Cadets will be instructed in a variety of
skills including drill, diplomacy and
security, and will have the opportunity
to support real marines in non-hostile
duties.

A CMC spokesperson described it as
an ideal spare-time activity for young
people, especially those who were
considering a career in the Corps.

However, there have been several
complaints from the Campaign for the
Demilitarisation of Society (CDS) that
the CMC is forcing militaristic
propaganda upon youngsters, and is
attempting to forge a race of
warmongers. The CMC spokesperson
declined to comment.

### Alien sighting or another debacle?

The CMC has deployed 2 squads from
4 Coy IV Field Regiment in the
Sharwood Forest region of Delors
continent, Bessieres, to investigate
reports be several groups of timber
workers of a strange creature seen in
the forest. The creature, described as
2.5 to 3 metres tall, pink with yellow
spots and bipedal, does not match any
known native lifeforms. CMC HQ has
refused to comment on any link
between this operation and the
embarassing debacle on Beta Hydri
earlier this year.

Hengists have eagerly seized upon this
report as a positive alien contact. They
have also identified the creature with
some of the Nabishu carvings found in
the Yokada desert on Chi Draconis.

The marines may have a long search
ahead of them, as Sharwood Forest
covers over 2000 sq km of dense
growth. During the Colonial War, an
army of resistance fighters took refuge
in the forest, and evaded capture
throughout the occupation by Earth
forces.
