It was a beautiful summer's day. Birds
were singing, butterflies danced through
the air, and children played under the
glowing sun. A perfect world you
might think, but look again! People
begin to cough, and start to shiver...
Babies choke on their own bile whilst
their parents stiffen by their cots. Death
is creeping through paradise...

It was to this supposedly peaceful
world - Altair IV - that I journeyed,
after a tip off that all was not well. My
crew and I were plunged straight into
action as we witnessed a running battle
between members of the terrorist
organisation known as The Brother-
hood, and 5 Company of the Colonial
Marine Corps, who had a training base
nearby.

However, even as we were filming,
the marines opened fire upon us,
without even so much as a challenge! I
received several painful direct blasts of
IR before they accepted that we were
civilians and ceased firing upon us.
There was no apology.

Apparently we were trespassing on
CMC property - funny that, as we had
walked through an open gate to get
here, I hate to think what would have
happened had a child wandered in ! -
and we were brutally man-handled off
the base, with no regard whatsoever to
our civil rights, or to our ID and papers
which were all in order and authorised.

Whilst they were paying so much
attention to kicking an innocent news
crew off their unguarded base, one of
the marines accidently set off a booby
trap, blowing his leg off and making
him writhe around in screaming agony.
However, Lt Gill and his men were
more interested in victimising us than
they were in Cpl Steele, who was
dying.

It took more than 20 minutes for proper
medical attention to arrive. The medics
who did finally wander in were not
properly equipped. Communications
were a shambles. Cpl Steele died.

One of the officers in charge, Lt
Harris, received radio communication
informing him of our authorisation from
a high ranking marine officer, but still
he insisted on keeping us away from
the truth.

We returned later in the day, to
discover a body that had been left lying
behind a barricade - it was a marine,
suffering from the effects of the plague,
but no effort was being made to treat
him, or to move him to a safe place.

When we were finally given access to
the inner compound, Lt Harris ordered
all the marines to run away and hide in
their barracks. Such bravery! And to
think these are the people we are meant
to rely on!

- **The Plague Cure**

We monitored the marine communica-
tions network, and listened in disbelief
as we heard a message being broadcast
from HQ to the base. The caller
identified herself as Sgn Lt Diamond,
who admitted to having gained unauth-
orised access to the channel, and was
trying to warn the marines that a
consignment of the drug needed to cure
the plague had already landed. It was a
full 5 minutes before she was acknowl-
edged.

Subsequently the marines were beaten
to the ship, and to the cure. The pilot
was missing. His body was discovered
a full 2 hours later, less than 20 meters
from the drop zone. Why such a fatal
delay?

We monitored the rebel communica-
tions closely. It would seem that the
main topics centered around the causes

- **FOOTNOTE**

As some of you may know, Altair IV
was one of the earliest frontier worlds
to be discovered, way before the
dangers of back-ground radiation were
known. Long-term exposure over the
generations has made the settlers
stronger and hardier, and has given
their skin a green pigmentation. They
are now known as the 'Urthankoi'.

It is estimated that over 40% of the
Urthankoi were killed by the plague,
and many of the survivors would seem
to have been left sterile. Medical
experts say that it is too soon to say if
this is a permanent effect.

The cause of the plague is still a
mystery.
