### CMC - Conspiracy of Laughter!
_Filed by: Krystal Spyers, March 2495_

What can only be described as strange
and 'fantastic' yet also disturbing
reports are filtering in following 5
Coy's last mission...

5 Coy had been dispatched to the
frontier world Connet, on a 'flag
waving' PR exercise, following the
planet's application to join the UW.
They were to be met by a delegation of
locals, and it is at this point that
conflicting accounts of events near
Little Dunnet-on-the-Marsh begin....

In statements taken after 5 Coy's
departure, the locals insist that they
waited for several hours for the
marines, and witnessed no strange or
unusual occurences. However, 5 Coy's
reports state that the locals were NOT
at the rendezvous point, and that they
did not arrive at the base until the
following afternoon, due to their
'detention' in a 'dimension gate'...

Confused? You will be... **and** it gets
better...!

**Knights in armour**

Several marines claim to have
witnessed knights (dressed in full
armour) battling each other with
swords. These knights of old were
impervious to Incapacitating Radiation
(I/R), and after brief skirmishes they
disappeared into puffs of mist!
However, one was later reported as
accompanying 5 Coy for a while, even
joining a patrol...

**Samuri**

Not only were knights in the area, but a
Japanese samurai was seen commiting
seppuku - an ancient ceremony of ritual
suicide, which has not been practised
for several centuries...

**Witches!**

Reports state that two **witches** were
arrested by the marines, and were
finally returned through the 'gate'
together with a self-confessed witch-
finder general called Peace. It is
claimed that these individuals were also
impervious to I/R, and that somehow
they controlled the 'gate'!

**Resurrection?**

To add to the 'fantastic' feel of events,
5 Coy's reports state that one of the
witches was killed by Marine Fugazi.
However this apparantly didn't stop her
from **getting up** in the morning to join
the marines for breakfast!

Marine Wellsley, who was declared
MIA on Grant's World, returned to 5
Coy in a semi-raving state. He is
reported as believing that he was the
infamous Duke of Wellington - of
whom he is a direct descendant.

Perhaps the strangest reports concern
members of an 'alternative' 5 Coy
attacking the marines. In addition to
several vicious large-scale fire-fights,
Zwalf reappeared (having also been
declared MIA on Grant's World) and
then attempted to assassinate Mexal,
and Oslo is reported to have
successfully knocked out
O'Shaunessy...

**Nasco & Diamond**

On a much more sinister note, one
report states that Nasco and Diamond
were also present on-world, and that
they were **both** dedicated to spreading
plague to eradicate all 'deviants'. 5 Coy
admit to failing to prevent the
detonation of a plague canister, which
contaminated the local water supply.

**'Deviants'**

The latest transferee into 5 Coy is a
half-Urthankoi - Kashell Durmas Sin
Klaytogin. Both he and Sgt Mexal
were reported as being specifically
targeted by this 'other' 5 Coy, and

Local administrators have lodged
complaints with the planetary

### Feel Good Factor?
_Filed by: Column Inches, March 2495_

Despite the general depression since the
Groombridge disaster, some of the big
corporations are starting to invest
heavily again.

**Nordberg Corp** have placed orders
for 4 'super-carriers'. Said to be more
than 5 times larger than any current
interstellar transporter, these represent
Nordberg's commitment to the rapid
growth in interstellar trade. After
intense bidding by 3 consortia, the
order was won by the consortium led
by Parnell and Aldsys Spaceyards.
Military Mercantile are said to be
"extremely disappointed" to have lost,
and have hinted that the decision was
swung by some large back-handers
rather than technical merit.

**Valen Pharmaceuticals** have brought
in specialist consultants D.F.Associates
to improve qulaity and productivity at
their plantations on Nyottonda.
Cultivation and harvesting of the
genetically engineered plants is labour
intensive, and it is hoped that
substantial savings can be achieved.

**Parnell** are to begin construction of a
new industrial city in Northvale,
Lumiere. A spokesman said that the
project would provide jobs and homes
for the many refugees from
Groombridge that the planet has
welcomed. Objections from
environmentalists were dismissed as
unfounded and obstructive, due to the
Maritime Cacti in question being
present throughout Lumiere's deserts. A
specialist consultancy firm is to be
retained to resolve any objections and
allay any fears from the locals -
although as all of Lumiere's inhabitants
are Parnell employees and their families
it is hard to see what objections they
might be tempted to raise.

### Other News

**Comms** troubles in some outlying
systems have provoked concerns that
the interstellar comms 'highways' might
not have been as infalible as was
previously thought. Investigations have
been launched into the claims that data
streams transmitted to corporate HOs
are being interrupted, resulting in either
serious delays or a complete loss of
transmission.

**Happy Haven** warden Sirac Le Conte
recently released sketchy details of the
sudden death of one of his patients.
Apparantly a power cell overload
coupled with a leaking therma-wash
electrified the sani-block floor. The
deceased was apparantly the first
unfortunate who wandered in without
his standard-issue rubber slippers.

This is the third 'accidental' death at
the Happy Haven on Dawn in the last
two years.

**Modern day pirates** are being blamed
for a series of hijacks and robberies
carried out over the last two months.
The UW have stepped up routine
patrols, although they are spread quite
thinly due to the concentration of troops
in the systems neighbouring Juliet.

**Rioting** has erupted again on several
penal colonies. Rumours of weapons
being smuggled to the colonies have
existed for some time, causing the
Penal Administrator to launch a heavy
crackdown. However reports arriving
from the trouble zones would seem to
indicate that he was unsuccessful.

### Card Robbery and Riots

The tradeable card game Magic hit the
news again yesterday when a shuttle
delivering a batch of cards to the
Hobygames Universe Store on
Braconis was hijacked by youths who
had been queuing overnight for the
latest release. A riot ensued with people
running off with armfuls of the cards
before a private law enforcement
agency contracted by the store restored
calm.

I spoke to Mr E Arios - a partner in the
Lone Star Security Firm - to find out
just what happened.

"It would appear that a certain element
of the crowd waiting for this delivery
had planned the raid on the shuttle.
Some kind of gas grenade was thrown
into the pilots compartment and a group
of youths proceded to force open the
rear doors and make off with the cargo.
Once this happened the whole crowd
attempted to get at the cargo. When
myself and my men arrived on the
scene we used what force was
necessary to break up the disturbance
and recover as much of our client's
stock as possible".

A spokesman for the games' producers
Wondergames informed us "obviously
we knew the game was going to be
popular, but even we did not expect
such a reaction. We are thinking of not
publicising the release dates of new
products until they are actually in the
shops, to prevent regrettable incidents
like that on Braconis."

As a result of the riots, the shuttle pilot
and his navigator were admitted to
hospital suffering from shock and eye
inflammation. 33 people received
medical attention for minor injuries
sustained as Lone Star attempted to
quell the riot.

### Obits

As reported earlier, last month saw
the deaths of two marines from 5
Coy.

**Mst Sgt Nix**

Mst Sgt Nix had just transferred into
5 Coy to take control of basic training
for recruits, following Cpt Zwarf's
disappearance on Grant's World last
January.

He was fatally shot 3 times by a lone
assailant armed with an illegal
projectile weapon.

The mission to Connet was his first,
and last, with 5 Coy.

**Marine Bean**

Marine Bean had been with 5 Coy
for over 4 years. He joined the CMC
on Altair IV, and was the last serving
member of Delta Squad.

Bean was also killed by three bullets
from a projectile weapon. He had
been taking part in a mission at night
attempting to prevent the detonation
of a plague cannister.

He was greatly admired by his fellow
marines, and will be sadly missed.
