### Ghost Ship Destroyed!
_Filed by: Krystal Spyers - CMC HQ, May 2495_

The missing space-liner SS Marie
Celesta was found _and_ destroyed last
month by 5 Coy. Most of the marines
escaped seconds before it exploded -
three of their number were not so
lucky...

In the middle of last month, the
Garavaglia (halfway through a major
re-fit) was conveying 20 members of 5
Coy back from a period of **R&R**.
However, just after finishing a jump
they found themselves in dangerously
close proximity to a derelict ship,
floating in space. The derelict was
identified as the SS Marie Celesta - a
cargo/passenger liner owned by
Haines-Worthy Starways.

5 Coy were dispatched by the
Garavaglia's captain Jacques to
investigate the ship and rescue any
surviving passengers. Due to onboard
security systems the marines were
unable to take torches or radios with
them, but they were issued with a
limited supply of chemical light sticks
and a fibre-optic comms cable provided
a connection back to the Garavaglia.

Reports from several members of 5
Coy state that the ship was littered with
bodies, but that many of the ship's
crew and passengers were still alive.
However, due to what has been
described as 'some form of jump
trauma' the survivors ranged from
being very confused to totally insane.
Some were armed, and engaged the
marines in several fire-fights in the
pitch-black as they searched the liner
room by room. To add to 5 Coy's
problems vast areas of the Marie
Celesta were rendered inaccessible due
to punctured airlocks or high rad levels.

The locks on every door had to be
manually bypassed - a task that could
only be performed by Tech Sgt Mexal
and Cpl Rocktansky. However the
doors were reported as automatically
closing every now and again, often
trapping unwary marines.

Marine Krinly managed to gain access
to the cargo manifest, and Tech Sgt
Mexal accessed several of the vessel's
other comp systems - one of which was
the JSE controller. Her tinkering
produced several results, the main one
being that she triggered off the JSE
Control Systems, which set the Marie
Celesta for one final cataclysmic jump -
one which would leave no survivors...

MFC Cassell and Marine Shepherd
were taken prisoner by a group of the
ship's former techs. Both were
assaulted and raped prior to their
rescue.

These renegade techs were led by a
sadistic madman known as 'The Tech'
who had control of the ship's remaining
operating systems - including life
support. He turned this off several times
in an attempt to force the marines to do
his bidding. However, Lt Oslo refused
to give in, and 'The Tech' was finally
captured after a heroic charge by Cpl
Beck.

The marines were forced into a
confrontation with several security
droids guarding one of the cargo bays.
Some were controlled by 'The Tech'
and some were malfunctioning, but
they were all eventually disabled - but
not before much panicking by the
marines.

Sgt B'Stard caused a minor stir in the
cargo bay when he casually tossed one
of the crates of toxic chemicals aside,
shattering their containers and filling the
bay with poisonous gas. Fortunately,
vital med drugs had just been
recovered, which enabled Cpls Silver,
Martins and Dibbley to treat their
wounded and also to temporarily
stabilise several of the passengers and
crew. (Incidentally, these same drugs
were used to treat Marine Wellesley

and enable him to get back into action
with 5 Coy for the first time since his
'appearance' as the Duke of Wellington
on Connet!).

Once treated, two of the passengers
were revealed as Ms Housenens (a
Haines-Worthy Starways Exec), and
Tech Systems Engineer Arnold ('The
Tech'). They entered their personal
security codes into the drive system, but
could not prevent the imminent jump.
With 5 minutes to go before jump-time
Lt Oslo gave the order to abandon ship.

Cpl Ratch was unable to bypass the
lock to the only operational EEV, as it
had been sabotaged by 'The Tech'.
However, having been stabilised as
Arnold he was able to undo his work
and unlock the door.

20 people managed to cram into the
EEV - it had been designed to cope
with only 12. However the 20 included
3 survivors from the ship, which left 3
marines to die in the subsequent
explosion of the Marie Celesta.

Sgt Trader and Marine Jarvis fought a
valiant rear-guard action, whilst Cpl
Ratch launched the EEV when he
realised that it was too full for him to
be saved, and that an explosion was
imminent.

The surviving 17 marines undoubtedly
owe their lives to these three.

The EEV was plucked from space by a
maintenance drone piloted by Cpl
Hunter - the marine pilot attached to the
Garavaglia's fleet crew.

The three non-marines rescued from the
Marie Celeste were Ms Housenens,
Tech Systems Engineer Arnold, and a
passenger only known as Slug.
Housenens and Arnold were collected
by a private Med Team several hours
after the EEV was recovered by the
Garavaglia.

A substantial quantity of creds was
recovered from the vessel by 5 Coy. A

It is understood that Lt Oslo has
applied for salvage rights for the EEV
and the creds recovered from the Marie

proceeds from this action will be used
to set up a fund for the relatives of
marines killed in action - of which the
last few months has seen a dearth.

Some blame for the deaths of 3 marines
and numerous passengers will
undoubtedly be laid on Mexal.
Although her actions set in motion the
chain of events that destroyed the
vessel it should be remembered that
without access to the computer, vital
med supplies could not have been
recovered. Without those supplies many
more marines would have died from

been able to access the EEV in those
last few minutes... of course if she
hadn't 'fiddled' around they wouldn't
have needed them in the first place...

5 Coy were sent in to rescue any
survivors from the SS Marie Celesta,
but they only came away with three.
Life-signs had been detected on several
other decks, and the marines' reports
details encounters with many more, but
only the one EEV was launched. With
such a heavy loss of life it is doubtful
that this mission could in any way be
deemed to have been a success.

**OBITS:** Trader and Jarvis were both on

Ratch, however, had been with 5 Coy
for over 2 years. He was a popular
member of the company, and will be
missed by many. None more so,
however, than Cpl Ruby Axe - it is
understood that they were to have been
married at the end of the year.

### Letters

We have received a letter from an
anonymous member of 5 Coy, heavily
criticising the actions of Lt Oslo - 5
Coy's CO during this excursion. He is
accused of incompetance, of
deliberately putting himself in danger -
and thereby risking his troops - and of
not being in communication with most
of his troops for most of the time. The
marine ended by questioning whether 5
Coy could survive without the
ex-Colonel Harris.

I contacted Lt Oslo and put the above
to him. His reply was as follows: "I
don't want to go into specifics
regarding a very difficult mission, but
anyone who would go bleating to the
press with this kind of story clearly
shows that they have no idea of how to
conduct themselves inside a military
unit. Any constructive criticism can
always be passed up the chain and no
officer worth his salt would reject such
out of hand. Incidently, like the late
Colonel Harris I have my strengths and
weaknesses, and his great strength was
that he knew his limitations, and as
such was always willing to listen to
sound advice. I'd like to think that I
follow in the same vein".

Cpl Beck of 5 Coy sent us a letter
regarding the security droids used by
HainesWorthy to protect sensitive areas
of the ship. Beck asked why the CMC
could not be issued with neutralising
devices to incapacitate such droids so
that the devastion wrecked by them
upon 5 Coy could could be avoided in
future. The answer is probably quite
simple - its' because if the CMC
guarded these devices with the same
degree of zeal as it does its drugs and
bombs, then the criminal underworld
would soon be overrun with them and
such droids would cease - of course -
to be any deterrent whatsoever!

### Errata

Marine Sabre of 5 Coy is alive and
well, and did not die following 5 Coy's
mission to Groombridge, as was stated
in last month's Interstella!

The report of his death was published
after his name was included in a list of
the deceased supplied by the CMC.

However, we would like to take this
opportunity to apologise for any distress
that we inadvertantly caused.

### Other News

**CIVIL UNREST** is brewing on the
'back-water' world Hal, according to
latest intel reports.

Hal's economy depends largely upon its
large slave workforce, and it would
appear that they are at last attempting to
throw off the yoke of oppression.

Hal is most probably remembered best
for the capture of an entire company of
marines (5 Coy - who else?).

**'SLUG'** - one of the three surviving
passengers from the SS Marie Celesta
has just issued a brief statement about
his voyage.

"Well, it was basically a good trip. The
sauna controls were on the blink, and
then the showers stopped working. It
was worth it for the tan, and for Janine
(the passenger rep) who was a babe.
The in-flight entertainment was
particularly life-like, and the EEV ride
was a good finish."

His psychiatric treatment continues.
