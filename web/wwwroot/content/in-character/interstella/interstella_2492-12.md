### Genetics Scandal
_Filed by: Krystal Spyers - SKN, Dec 2492_

The furore continues this month following the shock revelation that
certain corporations are using genetics to develop the 'ultimate
soldier' - a creature that is inhumanly fast and almost impervious to
I/R.

Although two corporations - Nas-Corp and Banordyne - are heavily
implicated in the affair, it is uncertain as to whether any hard
evidence of their direct involvement exists. Spokesmen for both
corporations in seperate press conferences both strongly denied
genetic manipulation, and both stated that the evidence against them
was purely circumstantial.

Reports are flooding in about the backlash of violence against the
scientific community, following the genetics scandal. Over 75 private
laboratories have been destroyed in various bomb attacks, none of
which have been even remotely linked with genetic research. It is
thought that these attacks are the work of several different groups.

Senor Ravaldez of the Zyatamosis Lab on Aurora lauched a bitter verbal
attack on the bombers, claiming that their hasty and ill-conceived
actions had destroyed years of valuable medical research into many
forms of disease. Whilst all notes held on comp-links were
automatically backed up off-site, any hand-written and audio notes had
been lost, together with many cultures and other such samples that
were vital to projects. He estimated that it would take at least 7
years to recover the ground lost as a result of the pointless
vandalism.

And Senor Ravaldez is not alone. His tale is very similar to that of
the other labs and research facilities who have become the outlet of
the fear and resentment targeted at scientists. UW spokesmen have
pleaded for calm, but how much good this will do is unknown.

Any illegal research is likely to be carried out in secret and highly
protected facilities, therefore attacks on known labs will probably
only affect legitmate projects. Such logic, unfortunately, is not
often recognised by the morally outraged...

### CMC Exam
_Filed by: Flovine Jerratt_

Last month saw the beginning of the annual CMC assessments of their
members. Exercises and mock missions are set up on a neutral planet
to test the efficiency and effectiveness of each company within the
CMC, so that training can be improved and targeted at areas in which
CMC personnel prove inadequate.

Among the companies who have recently undertaken this assessment is
5 Coy, whose exploits Interstella! has followed since the debacle on
Altair IV.

5 Coy's objective was apparantly to launch a real comms satellite at a
specific time - that is within the 1 hour window available. Missing
the deadline would mean that the launch of the satellite would have to
be delayed for a further 2 months.

Details of the problems set for 5 Coy have not been released, as not
all of the CMC forces have been through the test, but it has been
revealed that they were forced to execute several unplanned
manoeuvres. Vital components of the satellite were stolen from 5 Coy's
barracks, whilst they were out on an exercise. It has been alleged
that CMC HQ authorised the payment of a large amount of money to an
informant - in exchange for information on the stolen items - but this
has not yet been confirmed. The marines retrieved the parts after a
harrowing night mission, during which the marines were badly mauled -
at one point, only 2 marines were still conscious!

5 Coy finally succeeded in lauching the satellite, but we will have to
wait until the full report is published to see how well they
performed in the eyes of the invigilators!

### Conflict Spreads
_Filed by: Krystal Spyers_

The war on Groombridge has finally spilled over into neighbouring
systems. Ships loyal to the various factions in the dispute have been
wrecking havoc in the space around Groombridge, disrupting both
merchant lanes and civilian traffic alike.

The inhabitants of Beti Hydri are said to be slightly unnerved by the
proximity of these mini space-battles to their own system, although
it is not thought likely that the conflict will spread quite that far
- at least for some months yet!

The situation on planet remains somewhat unclear, but already
atrocities have been comitted. During the simultaneous bombing of
shelters in both Chidan and Kasdunni two months ago, over 500,000
citizens were killed. Among the dead that night were 12 volunteer
med-teams, and three quarters of the Intergalactic Press Corps
stationed on-world - the rest were mainly women and children. That
such pointless slaughter should be wrought against both of the main
combatants leads to the disturbing conclusion that a third party is
anxious to prolong the war. But there are no obvious suspects...

According to reports received recently, religious involvement has
increased to such a degree that the One True Church is sending in a
number of its own troops, to 'protect its interests'. A spokesman for
the OTC stated that its troops had been instructed to act in
self-defense only. Representatives of the Future Party have however
claimed that the OTC is intending to take the opportunity during the
confusion of the hostilities to persecute those of differing beliefs.
The FP also denied claims that it is responsible for the recent spate
of ritual-style killings.

Also heavily involved in the conflict is the Freedom for Chidan
Movement, which has lauched several guerilla-style attacks on
government forces. The Groombridge authorities have denied that the
Groombridge Province Pact had been shattered by the war, but refused
to comment on the escalation of conflict from the Chidanian region to
the rest of the world.

The level of corporate involvement is as yet unknown. Both Military
Mercantile and Network Systems are based on-world, and both yesterday
confirmed that they had taken steps to move the base of their
operations to new locations for an unspecified period of time.

Various mercenary forces are understood to have been attracted to
Groombridge by the prospect of paid action, but who - if anyone - has
employed such units is not known.

The flood of refugees from the region has been suddenly cut off as the
action has spread across the main routes from the area. Air travel is
still possible, but carries its own very obvious risks.
