### Universal Exclusive!
_Filed by: Krystal Spyers - Groombridge, Feb 2493_

Whilst covering action with 4 Coy of the CMC in the so-called neutral
zone in the Chidian/Kasdunni region of Groombridge, I made contact
with an unusual eye-witness to the actions of 5 Coy. Honell, a young
mother and her 8 week old son, had just been picked up by a CMC
dropship following their escape from the zone under heavy fire.

She had arrived at a mercenary enclave, to find that a new bunch of
'recruits' had arrived. "I knew there was something wrong with them,
as no-one could possibly be as stupid as they seemed! But you soon
learn not to ask the wrong questions here".

"My trade is information - I exchange it for food, equipment and safe
passage. There's a network of IB's (Info Brokers) throughout the zone,
and we know more about what's going on than the authorities do."

Before going to an extensive CMC debrief, I prompted Honell for more
news of the marines from 5 Coy. She spoke highly of 'Decker' (Captain
Harris), and matter-of-factly about being knocked unconscious by a
marine sergeant. There was no resentment on her part about the
assault, indeed she gave the impression that it was by no means an
unusual occurance.

**PARIS DEAD!**

This same marine sergeant had boasted openly about having 'taken out'
one of the mercs who was apparantly well known to 5 Coy. From Honell's
description and local rumours, it would appear to have been Cpl Paris
- formerly captain of 5 Coy - who was wanted for charges including
incitement to use projectile weapons and involvement in the attempted
genocide of the Urthankoi.

**CANNIBALISM**

Honell revealed that food supplies in the area are so low that people
have been forced to eat the bodies of the recently deceased. "I have a
child to think of, and I'd do anything to ensure his health. I didn't
enjoy it, but after a while you just get used to it. We did tell the
marines they were eating a member of 4 Coy, but I think they thought
we were joking!"

Sources within the CMC have confirmed that several members of 5 Coy
have undergone tests on their stomach contents. The results have not
yet been released, but undenied rumours claim that several tested
positive.

Over 50 members of 4 Coy are listed as missing in action on
Groombridge to date.

Later reports on 5 Coy's progress suggest that they did not complete
their mission, and that several marines were left behind when they
withdrew prematurely from the zone.

_I wonder what will have happened to their bodies?_

Regular readers of Interstella! will know that I have not always
agreed with the methods used by 5 Coy. I am also known for my mixed
feelings about vigilanties and those who take the law into their own
hands. However, on the matter of the possible murder of the former
captain Paris, I would comment that at least one of Nasco's associates
has been punished for his crimes!

### Doctor Verdict

Lt O'Shaunessy of 2 Coy has been found guilty of malpractice and
membership of the League Against Warfare (LAW) by a CMC courtmartial.
As well as dishonourable discharge from the Corps, the malpratice
charge carries a minimum sentence of 10 years. Although the CMC have
not yet announced where O'Shaunessy will serve her time, it will
probably be on one of the infamous moon bases.

As to the second charge, membership of LAW is not yet illegal for
members of the public, but it is an offence as regards members of the
CMC and other UW organisations, and carries a minimum sentance of 4
years.

Sentancing is expected later this month.

### Winno's Stolen

Thieves have struck a mortal blow to the Groombridge Planetary
Exhibition Centre's hopes of showing the complete works of the
legendary artist Winno. Her most famous painting "Space Fatigue" has
disappeared from the vault's of the First Bank of Groombridge. The
rest of the collection was being shipped in from Sol, but the
transporter has been recalled following news of the theft.

When asked why the original paintings were to be displayed rather
than following the usual trend of using hologramatic representations,
the PEC's director stated that the exhibition of the originals was to
have been a 'coup' for the centre, in celebration of its fiftieth
anniversary.
