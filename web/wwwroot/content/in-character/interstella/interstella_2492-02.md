### Crooked Colonel Cleared.

Justice took a near mortal blow yester-
day, when Colonel Nasco, recently
arrested by the CMC, was cleared of
all the charges laid against him. The list
included:

- Conspiracy in the attempted
  genocide of the population of
  Altair IV.
- Unauthorised use of CMC
  equipment
- Accepting bribes, most
  notably from De Witt Mining
- Assault of at least six
  inhabitants of the Luthusian
  District of Chi Draconis
- Use of projectile weapons
- Resisting arrest

A spokesman for the prosecution team,
still in shock following the surprise
acquital, said "We don't understand
how this can have happened. The
evidence against the Colonel was over-
whelming, and we were in no doubt
whatsoever that he was headed for a
long term in prison. The Judge's
decision to throw virtually all our
evidence out of court is beyond belief.
Thank God it's his last case before
retirement!"

The defense team's comments were
unsurpisingly predictable, smugly
declaring that the result proved their
client's innocence, and that he was
relieved to have cleared his name.

He was stated as having been a target
for both his superiors - who were
worried about his rise through the ranks
\- and lower ranking officers, who were
desperate for promotion. The prime
movers in this conspiracy to blemish
his record, were named as 5 Company,
who were initially under his command.

When I tried to contact the Judge, I
was told that he'd already left the
planet to begin a lengthy pleasure
cruise around the colonies. His office
refused to release any details of his
schedule.

According to the record, Colonel
Nasco was a career soldier, a highly
decorated and undoubtedly brave man,
and being one of the youngest full
Colonels in the CMC he had been
destined to go far. However, his rapid
ascent through the ranks was abruptly
halted last month when he was arrested
for offences against humanity by 5 Coy
of the CMC.

When I asked about Nasco's future, a
CMC spokesman refused to comment,
and neither was he any more forth-
coming about any action that would be
taken following the acquital. My
sources reveal that the CMC are as
shocked as the prosecution team, but
being upholders of the law they have
no choice but to abide by the Judge's
decision, however ludicrous it may be!
:::

::: column-right
De Witt Mining, were also implicated
in the Altair IV slaughter, but when
questioned a spokesman denied all
knowledge of the affair. When I asked
about Nasco he commented that: "The
Colonel is a hardworking loyal officer
of the Colonial Marines, who we have
dealt with over the last few years on a
purely professional basis. In all respects
we have found him to be a completely
honest and trustworthy man, whom we
are glad to see cleared of the charges
brought against him."

I was present in court when the
evidence against the Colonel was pre-
sented, and can see no valid reason for
its dismissal. Nasco has escaped retri-
bution for these most henious of
crimes, and this must surely rate as the
greatest miscarriage of justice of all
time. I share the prosecution team's
disbelief and shock, that a murderer is
allowed to walk free from court thanks
to one senile old man.

How many pieces of silver did they
pay you, Judge Judas?

KRYSTAL SPYERS
