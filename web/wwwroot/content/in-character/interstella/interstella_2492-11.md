### Genetics Scandal
_Filed by: Krystal Spyers - SKN, Nov 2492_

The UW and scientific world are in an uproar following the recent
discovery that certain companies have been engaging in highly dubious
and illegal genetic research.

Apparantly the first rumours of gentic tinkering surfaced in August
2491, on Insalon III. Locals were recruited by Nas-Corp - a major
corporation - to raid rival company Banordyne's field testing station,
with an aim to stealing research notes and samples. It is unclear as
to how successful this initial raid was, as the whole site was later
destroyed by Nas-Corp.

The recent upturn in events occured in early October, when Banordyne
would appear to have launched a retaliatory strike against Nas-Corp's
main research facilities. The R & D labs were totally devastated by a
massive explosion, apparantly caused by a bomb planted by Banordyne
troops to cover their actions and further 'inconvenience' Nas-Corp.

The Banordyne troops then made good their escape, with several genetic
samples and what is rumoured to have been a fully developed specimin.
Unfortunately for them, they were hailed by a patrolling UW ship,
which gave chase when the fleeing vessel refused to reply, let alone
stop.

A report submitted by the UW ship's captain would seem to indicate
that the as-then-unidentified vessel maintained a very high speed,
which could not be beaten by his ship, despite it being equipped with
what was supposedly the latest in engine technology.

After a prolonged hunt, the Banordyne vessel performed a desperate
manoeuvre through an asteroid belt in an attempt to dislodge pursuit,
with fatal results - his ship was struck and plummeted into the
atmosphere of a nearby planet, burning up such that no sizeable pieces
of wreckage reached the surface.

This appeared to be the end of the matter, as the UW Ship presumed its
quarry had been obviously involved in some kind of illicit dealings,
but they'd seen it be totally destroyed. The case was closed...

The story is taken up about a week later by a judge from the planet,
who whilst out walking saw a bipedal figure moving at an impossible
speed. Having no idea of the chase that recently occured above his
planet, his first thought was that it must have been an alien. Not
wishing to cause panic, or ruin his credibility should it prove to be
a false alarm, he quickly but quietly reported his sighting to the UW
delegation on planet.

5 Coy of the CMC was immediately dispatched to the scene, to
investigate the reported alien sighting. It is now known that a
Banordyne recovery team also landed, covertly followed by a Nas-Corp
strike-force.

Although the marines sent to the planet have been kept out of public
for extensive debriefing, the following details were obtained from an
initial report forwarded to CMC Central Command. The marines
interviewed the judge who had first witnessed the 'alien' together
with a local 'trapper' who accused the marines of stealing from his
traps. It appears that the 'alien' had been feeding on his catches.

The marines intercepted radio comms from both the undercover corporate
teams, several members of which would appear to have been
extraordinarily inept.

The 'alien' was finally cornered by the marines, who were forced to
destroy it with the help of the trapper. Hopefully further details
will soon be released by the CMC.

The most disturbing aspect of this creature would appear to have been
its high resistance to I/R. As the creature has been carefully
genetically designed and grown, it can only be presumed that certain
corporations are seeking to create the ultimate soldier, but with what
purpose in mind?

The public outcry has exceeded all previous levels, and I feel that
the protests have only just begun. An immediate inquiry has been
launched by the UW,

But just how wide-reaching will the consequences of this affair be?
Initial predictions are that much tougher laws will be passed
governing genetic research, with greater powers being granted to those
enforcing the laws. Assets of both Nas-Corp and Banordyne have been
frozen, and their Directors have been removed for questioning. It is
also understood that CMC forces have been drafted in to all of their
facilities.

One disturbing aspect is that genuine genetic research may suffer as a
result of this abominable behaviour, but then who can say what is
scientific progress and what is playing 'god'? The Puritans have
condemned such genetic tinkering for years, and now public opinion is
with them all the way.

Greed and a lust for power are human emotions that would appear to
breed monsters as surely as any scientific research... Were they
playing god or helping mankind?...

### News in Brief

**THE** Puritans have issued a statement strongly denouncing all
genetic research. As well as citing the "twisted mad scientist
routines practised by greedy corporate power-mongers" they also
criticise the activities of one of the more glamorous genetics
researchers - Dr Rachel Cantrell. Following the announcement last
month of the birth in September of her and Torboran Haze's son, the
Puritans claim that Dr Cantrell altered the child's genetic structure
whilst still in the womb, to produce the 'perfect' baby.

If this is the case, then Dr Cantrell has risked much to please the
man she loves. The Puritans state that such alterations are for
vanity's sake, and are a blatant misuse of power. "Should such
'creations' be allowed, and where do you stop? What is the difference
between making monsters and supposedly 'perfect' children? Is Dr
Cantrell a Hilterite, intent on manufacturing the 'master race'?"

The Puritans have lodged an official complaint about Dr Cantrell's
activities with the UW, together with a request that young
Haze-Cantrell be declared non-human, with the same classification as a
clone...

**THE** existence of clones is also being questioned in the light of
the genetics scandal, with some calling for tighter controls on
production. The main fear is that unscrupulous researchers may be
tempted to genetically alter clones in an attempt to breed better
fighters/killers. Scare-mongers are saying that the human race will
eventually be wiped out by any such 'superior' beings.

**SEVERAL** research establishments have been fire-bombed in the last
few days, although it is as yet unclear whether they were engaged in
genetic research, or were just victims of the backlash of fear and
loathing.

**MARINES** are rumoured to have been involved in an attack outside a
nightclub recently. There are no further details as yet, but it is
believed that there may be a connection with some very powerful and
illegal narcotic substances.

**TALKING** of marines, Captain Gillham of 5 Coy has been keeping a
very low profile recently. Apparantly he has not yet rejoined 5 Coy
following his kidnapping on Groombridge... And he's not the only one.
Lt Katz his adjutant has not returned from her enforced visit to CMC
Central Command earlier in the year! What do 5 Coy do with their
officers?!!!

Most of this issue has been taken up with the momentous scandal
surrounding the genetics world, but life has gone on elsewhere too!
