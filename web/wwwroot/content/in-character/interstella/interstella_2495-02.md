### The Juliet Affair
_Filed by: Krystal Spyers, Jan 2495_

We were forced to pull last month's
report on the 'Juliet Affair' - on the
advice of our Legal Team - following
UW threats of closing us down
**PERMANENTLY**. However, their
'blanket' injunction expired on 31st
Jan, leaving us free to 'speculate' as to
just what lengths the UW may have
been prepared to go to just to cover
their tracks!

Our 'tale' begins early in December last
year...

Imagine if you will a 'meteor' striking
the surface of the anti-UW planet
Juliet. The planet's not-insignificant
body of police and security forces
cordons off the area and begins a
systematic search...

Members of 5 Coy are recalled
suddenly from annual leave for a
'training mission'...

The Garavaglia is brought out of
dry-dock and prepped for action...

Shortly thereafter the Garavaglia is
orbiting Juliet. The planetary authorities
complain to the UW...

Rumours abound on Juliet that off-
world troops are in the area...

The 'meteor' is found to have crashed
at the bottom of a lake...

Divers discover that the meteor is really
a manned observation craft - a
spy-in-the-sky. Most of the crew are
dead, however two are unaccounted
for...

Members of the salvage team are
ambushed by a body of troops as they
return to their base. Two are captured,
the others walk away when the troops
are not looking! (Does such
incompetance sound familiar?)

One of the prisoners is rescued, and
later claims that he was systematically
tortured by the troops. He states that
before being rescued he heard some of
them refer to each other as
O'Shaunessy, Waylander, Ruby,
Apone, Calvin... the one called
Waylander even wore a security collar
- just like the Waylander of 5 Coy - but
it didn't stop him from hacking his
prisoner's little fingers off...

His description of one - which included
"huge pointy ears" - matches that of
Marine Mexal, also of 5 Coy.

He also states that he heard his
companion being executed in the next
room, shortly afer which he hears them
say "we'll have to get rid of him..."

A local ranger is found dead after being
shot at close range. His pregnant wife
unknowingly chats to the troops who
killed him, and later is able to identify
one of the female soldiers as matching
the description of Ruby Axe of 5
Coy...

His three children have lost their father,
and a elderly widow has lost her only
son...

One of the crew from the 'spy-ship' is
captured by security forces. His
description matches that of Captain
Zwalf of 5 Coy...

CMC HQ refuses to reveal Zwalf's
current location, or where he has been
over the last few weeks...

A jacket is found in the area of the
'meteor' crash bearing CMC insignia...

At 5 Coy's Annual Regimental Dinner
it is announced that Marines Zeus and
Cassel are promoted to MFC for
bravery during action...

What action?

Weren't 5 Coy on a training mission...

So what are we suggesting?

That the UW had a manned craft
spying on the anti-UW planet Juliet,
which after some malfunction crash-
landed onto the planet? That the UW
would want so hard to cover its tracks
(and prevent an intergalactic incident)
that it would endorse arson, torture and
murder? That the troops sent in to do
the dirty work were none other than the
seemingly-reformed 5 Coy?

Now would we do that...

FOOTNOTE:

Last month Sgt Chaos was removed
from the Officer Training List, and Sgt
Martins was demoted to Cpl. The
reason for these was given as 'errors in
paperwork' - but is it just coincidence
that these marines were both amongst
the list of marines accused by the Juliet
authorities of the torture and murder of
at least one of its citizens...

It will be interesting to see if Sgt
Apone's paperwork is in order when
**HE** returns to duty...

### Time Gates?
_Filed by: Krystal Spyers, Jan 2495_

Strange reports have filtered back from
Grants World of mysterious 'gates'
leading to what is rumoured to be the
past and/or future!

Grant's World is one of the proposed
new homes of many of the refugees
from the doomed planet of
Groombridge. A UW-contracted
Colony Prep Team were just over a
third of the way through their 3 year
term when they reported a series of
accidents, missing equipment, and
sabotage, culminating in the loss or
disappearance of 5 of the 10 strong
team.

A Colonial Survey Team sent to
investigate the reports disappeared,
paving the way for 5 Coy's first public
appearance since the Juliet Affair.

5 Coy eventually found that the
majority of the strange events occuring
on Grant's World were due to two
groups of Hengists, but many others
were accompanied by strange clouds of
mist, whistling noises and loud bangs.

The Hengist movement itself had
recently come to believe that Grant's
World was to be the site of the 'second
coming' of the aliens who created the
galaxy and founded the human race.

The first Hengist group were content
with stealing equipment and acts of
sabotage, and they were led by a
member of the Prep Team itself - Jarvis
Bryant. Three of his former team
members - Ilsa Transk, Kerry Regan
and the group leader [Arn?] Holtzman -
had to be forcibly restrained from
harming Bryant when he was
eventually released.

The second group was made up of
radical activists, who took any
measures they thought necessary to
drive the Prep Team from the planet.
They were also responsible for heavy
attacks on 5 Coy. Their leader was
none other than C J Farqhuar, who
admitted to 5 Coy that he had lost
control of the group. Shortly after
leaving the marines he was vapourised
by a closing 'gate'.

5 Coy's investigations led them to
deduce that the strange mists, whistling
sounds and loud cracks occured when
one of these gates was either just
opening or closing. Anyone or anything
caught in the gate at that time was
instantly reduced to its/their component
molecules - the only trace of their
passing being a fine white powder. It is
believed that the CSD shuttle was
caught in an opening gate.

However, safe passage was possible
through the gate either way whilst it
was open. 5 Coy witnessed a strange
soldier in unfamiliar garb suddenly
appear, and fire indicriminately before
vanishing into the mist. Reports also
state that a suspected cybernetic trooper
and accompanying tech safely used the
gate.

One of the missing Prep Team
members returned through one of the
gates after an absence of several weeks,
but he has since been certified clinically
insane...

During one of the firefights, Marines
Ricottanini and Wellesly were captured
by the Hengists. 5 Coy were forced to
stage a last minute rescue attempt as the
activists tried to take their prisoners
through an open gate.

Ricottanini managed to escape, but
Captain Zwalf leapt through the gate
just as it was closing in what turned out
to be a futile attempt to rescue Wellsley
from the Hengists. Neither returned.
Samples were taken of the powder
scattered around the area, but no human
traces were found. It is possible that
Zwalf and Wellesly could return to our
own 'time', but whether they would
still be sane cannot be guaranteed.

Due to the strange phenomena, Grant's
World has been 'sealed off' by the UW
and the Colony Prep Team have been
removed. The UW have been forced to
pay the CPT their full contract value.

One of the CPT has since lodged a
complaint with CMC HQ about an
alledged assault perpetrated by Marine
Mexal. Ms Kelly Regan claims that
Mexal stabbed her in the ear during a
brief struggle. A spokeperson for the
CMC said that an internal inquiry had
been launched, and that no further
comment could be made until its
findings were known.

### Obits?

Captain Zwalf, 32, was underage when
he joined the CMC back in 2478, and
had been responsible for 5 Coy's Basic
Training Program since before the
evacuation of Altair IV in 2491. Often
leading his troops from the front, he
had been wounded in action on many
occasions. He has been accused of
sexism, but no charges were ever
brought. He rose to Major but was
busted down to Mst Sgt - the reason
has never been made public.

Marine Arthur Wellsley, 28, was the
last in the Wellington line and holder of
the title Duke. He had been with 5 Coy
for just over a year after passing his
Basic Training at the second attempt.

Both marines are officially listed as
MIA.

### New craze sweeps galaxy!
_Filed by: Ivor Throwt, Feb 2495_

A tradable card game called 'Magic'
has taken the galaxy by storm since its
launch at the 425th Games Fair on
Alpha Centauri. The game - which
involves using combinations of cards to
produce a winning deck - sold out
within hours of its release, and some
shops have had to hire guards to protect
deliveries.

Kieth Hornsblow, president of the
Marquee Models chain said yesterday
"I've never seen such excitement over a
game before. Even when they released
Nuns n Guns we never had a reaction
like this".

The OTC has taken a dim view of the
game. A spokesman said "We believe
that this and other role-play games
corrupt the moral fibre of our youth and
encourage heathen and satanistic
worship."

Will Magic be more than just a flash in
the pan? Does it corrupt the youth of
today? I'll keep you posted!

### Refugee Turmoil

Trouble is brewing in several of the
Groombridge refugee settlements
throughout the worlds.

The New Light organisation is reported
to be deliberately stirring up trouble in
mixed settlements - where Chidanians
and Kasdunni are living together.
Inflamatory meetings are held, leading
to buildings being torched, and people
being seriously assaulted.

A riot struck one such settlement in the
Harfen region of Dawn - which before
the infiltration of agitators had been a
model of harmonious co-existence.

No-one has yet been charged with
incitement, but it is widely believed that
undercover UW operatives have been
working on Dawn for some time now,
in an effort to apprehend those
responsible.

Some refugees have been persuaded by
members of New Light to return to
their doomed homeworld. The UW has
strongly urged people not to go down
to the surface, stating that it cannot
insure the safety of anyone returning to
a planet which is still being torn apart
by earthquakes, volcanic eruptions and
continental shifts.

Another site of a refugee settlement is
in the news, but not for reasons of
internal strife. A UW Court judgement
is expected shortly over a contested
'property' claim on the planet Heurls.
Those vying for ownership are De Witt
Mining, Iowa Power and the local
people of Sheeple.
