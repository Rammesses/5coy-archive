### Urthankoi Revenge

Last month, 5 Coy of the CMC
uncovered evidence that the Urthankoi
Government were buying components
for a deadly artificial virus. It is
believed that this was to be used in a
revenge attack against those who
unleashed the deadly plague that
ravaged Altair IV last year. These
chemicals were to be supplied by Jaz
Matlocke, Third Under-Manager of the
Erasmus Corporation plant on Eta
Ceptai, who was later arrested by 5
Coy for various offences, including
attempted murder.

In a bizarre twist of fate, the govern-
ment representive, a diplomat named
Draksfor Kev-Lin, was apparantly
murdered before completing the deal,
by another Urthankoi, a thief who had
planned to assume his identity and
escape from the planet undetected.

What followed would appear to have
been a chase after the Urthankoi by
members of 5 Coy, who thought they
were rescuing a kidnapped diplomat;
and also the Ricottanini Family, from
whom the thief had stolen what is
reported to be items of substantial
value.

Reports from 5 Coy indicate that they
'rescued' the Urthankoi, and then
released him to a couple of Erasmus
Corporation Techs, whom he later
murdered. It was also reported that 7
marines were killed on the planet,
although whom exactly this was due to
is not clear. Several supposed members
of the League Against Warfare (LAW)
were apprehended by 5 Coy, for
offences under the Terrorism Acts.

Attempts were made to contact the
reknowned Ricottanini Family, but it
was announced that they were in
mourning for one of the Family, who
died recently on Eta Ceptai. Gino
Ricottanini was 15, and it is understood
that he was killed by members of 5
Coy, whilst he was escorting the thief
back to a dropship. All the Head of the
Family would say is that he bore no
malice towards 5 Coy, the debt had
been repaid, and Captain Gillham (the
Commanding Officer) was a man with
whom he could do business.

**JUST IN:** An anonymous source from
within 5 Coy has revealed that their
former doctor, Lt Sgn Tane Diamond,
has been missing since their evacuation
of Altair IV, when the infamous
Colonel Nasco ordered her to go off
planet with him. Apparantly evidence
later uncovered has revealed that
Nasco, a Puritan, planned to murder
Diamond, due to her genetic dif-
ferences. A CMC spokesman refused
to comment.

### Aliens?

The intergalactic grapevine is sizzling
today with rumours that New Worlds
have deliberately covered up evidence
proving that humans are not the only
race to inhabit this universe. It is said
that the Company destroyed many alien
artifacts so that their terraforming of the
as yet unnamed world would not be
interrupted. New Worlds have declined
to officially comment, although one
Exec questioned did say that "those
loonies in the Hengists would claim
anything for publicity".

### Revolution?

It is reported that the growing spate of
terrorist attacks occuring on Sirius have
continued today, breaking the truce
offered by Baron Lukel, whose family
have ruled the planet since its colonisa-
tion in 2398. The terrorists claim that
they and their families are living under
an oppressive regime, whose secret
police are responsible for many crimes
against humanity. A United Worlds'
spokesman said today that it was not
UW policy to interfere with the govern-
ment of worlds, and reiterated that
terrorism was a crime that would not be
tolerated. "They should further their
case around the negotiation table, and
not resort to violent means". Our
reporter was refused permission to go
planetside, due to reporting restrictions
imposed by the Barony.

### Colonel Resigns

Those who followed the Nasco trial last
month may be interested to hear that
the Colonel - who was aquitted of all
charges - has now resigned from the
CMC, and has taken up a directorship
with De Witt Mining. Spokesmen for
both Nasco and the company declined
to comment.

### Fraud

The investigation into the Intergalactic
Swindle continues, with the UW Team
concentrating their inquiries on two
planets, Sol and Tremus Dex. All
banking and business transactions from
and to these worlds for the last 5 years
are being sifted through, and all
accounts have been frozen. Those
affected by the freeze are requested to
contact the UW Team, who will review
each case individually. It is understood
that several arrests may be imminent.
