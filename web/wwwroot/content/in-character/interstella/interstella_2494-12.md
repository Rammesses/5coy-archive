### 5 COY ON HOLIDAY

Members of 5 Coy - which has not
seen active duty since its mission to
X243 last June - took the opportunity
to celebrate their recent release from
quarantine by holidaying on Sol.

Olde Earth - as the Olde Earth Tourist
Authority (OETA) prefer to call it - has
seen a huge increase in the number of
tourists over the last year, most of
whom are satisfying a life-long
ambition to see where "it all started".

The marines toured the ancient city of
Hoods-Nottingham, and with them
went their latest addition recently
transferred in - Ms Gina Ricottanini.
Bystanders reported considerable
friction between Ms Ricottanini and
(Lt) Calvin, which is probably
understandable considering that 7
marines and one of the Ricottanini's -
Gina's brother - were killed in a
pitched battle between the two on Eta
Ceptai back in Feb 2492.

The tour included public ground-
transport, visits to the Caves, the
famous Tales of Robin Hood, the
Castle, the oldest pub 'The Trip to
Jerusalem', the public toilets, exotic
shops and the Mega-zone. (Act Sgt)
Chaos and (Cpl) Silver kept each other
constant company throughout the tour,
fuelling speculation that this may be
more than just a holiday romance! Also
enjoying their new found freedom from
quarantine were (Sgt) Martins, Fugazi,
Ratch and Axe. Joining those from 5
Coy was (Cpl) Jackson from CMC
Admin.

On being asked about the tour, OETA
rep Marion [Themaid?] said "Hoods-
Nottingham is an ideal centre, with
something for all ages. We were lucky
this tour, they were running one of the
Theme 500 Specials, and we were able
to experience the old city just as it was
500 years ago. The attention to detail is
incredible - there's genuine litter in the
streets, and the public toilets in the
Market Square are quite simply
breath-taking. It's an experience not to
be missed!"

### SCIENTIST MISSING

Concern has been mounting for the
safety of a top-level research scientist,
after he failed to return to his post as
Head of Special Projects at Blackwell
Power from a period of annual leave.

Professor Thom Merrilion had been on
the same tour of Hoods-Nottingham as
the off-duty marines, but has not been
seen since.

Blackwell Power have denied reports
that Professor Merrilion sold a valuable
prototype from a top secret project to
an exec from Malenski Transportation.
However, insiders from the company
have said that there has been much
frenzied activity in his labs lately, with
scientists and techs frantically searching
the premises to try to determine if
anything has been taken.

A spokesperson for Malenski Transpor-
tation confirmed that one of their execs
- Frazer Johnson - was on the same
tour, but that it had been booked
months in advance. He laughed off the
rumours of a secret transaction, but said
that his company would not hesitate to
investigate any evidence suggesting that
their exec had acted illegally.

### UW Rep Resigns

Claudia Goldine, the former UW Rep
to Hal, has resigned from the
Diplomatic Corps.

Popular rumour has it that she is taking
up a post with D.F. Associates, the

### Alien Hoaxer Caught

Mr Thille Edmonds has been arrested
by 4 Coy IV Field Regiment, whilst on
operations in Sharwood Forest, Delors
continent, and is to be charged with
deliberately wasting C.M.C. resources
and time.

Edmonds was captured in a night
ambush whilst dressed in a padded
yellow pink-spotted costume. He has
admitted responsibility for the so-called
alien sightings in the region, which he
said he did for "a bit of a lark".

Edmonds claimed that the alien suit
was based on pictures which have been
in his family for generations, and that
he had been inspired by the sightings of
an alien on Beta Hydri.

A spokesman for the Hengists said that
while they were deeply disappointed
that this time there had been no alien,
they were still very eager to inspect Mr
Edmonds pictures as they could be
proof of alien visitors to Earth as early
as the 21st century...

### DE WITT TO SUE DIAMOND

Speculation is mounting about the
possibility of the De Witt Mining
Corporation suing Sgn Lt Diamond of
5 Coy.

De Witt is reported to have consulted
its extensive legal team over remarks
made by her in an interview published
in Interstella! last month.

In the interview with Krystal Spyers,
Diamond alledged that De Witt was in
league with ex-colonel Nasco, and that
crimes committed by other former
employees had the backing of the
corporation.

A spokesperson for the CMC refused
to comment on the reports.

### LOTTERY IS 500 YEARS OLD!

Last month saw celebrations to mark
the 500th anniversary of the launch of
the now legendary UK National
Lottery.

The UK (United Kingdom) was a
nation in its own right at the time, but
later became part of United Europe.

500 years ago the lottery was one
amongst many, but from such humble
beginnings it rose to become the
biggest and most successful lottery of
all time!

Legend even has it that the early
Parnell ancestors gained their fortune as
a result of a lucky ticket...

The Intergalactic Lottery Co. has
launched a new Ad campaign, hoping
to hype up ticket sales in the wake of
the 500th Anniversary celebrations.
