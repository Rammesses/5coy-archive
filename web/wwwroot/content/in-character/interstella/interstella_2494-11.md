### Know your Marines - Diamond

To continue this series, I've spoken this
month to Sgn Lt Diamond of 5 Coy,
recently rescued from X243.

**So Sgn Lt, how long have you
been in the Corps?**

I joined up in 2487, and spent 3 years
in Intel before transferring into 5 Coy
in March '90.

**What did you do before you
enlisted?**

I travelled extensively with my family -
my father's in the Diplo Corps so we
had to move with his assignments -
before spending 4 years at the
Acadamy.

**So why did you join the CMC?**

When I was 15 I was rescued by a
CMC special forces unit from a group
of terrorists who were holding me
hostage. I joined the marines because I
wanted to repay the debt, so that I
could save lives too.

**Why did you join under an
assumed name?**

I wanted to succeed or fail on my own
merit - not because senior officers were
trying to suck up to my father!

**Why did you become a Med Tech?**

To save lives.

**Why did you leave 5 Coy on Altair
IV back in July 2491?**

I think that's already on public record -
Nasco ordered me to accompany him
off-planet, I had no choice.

**Yet you were in radio contact with
5 Coy later on that very same day?**

As their records state, before Nasco's
flagship left orbit I was helping their
Med Techs to identify the virus.

**Why did it take so long to find?**

I wasn't allowed to return to the planet
to help, and then as we were analysing
samples of the virus the ship's Med
Systems 'failed'.

**How do you explain the
subsequent delivery cock-up?**

It wasn't accidental - the cure was
deliberately sent down without an
escort into a hostile zone, and I was
expressly forbidden from contacting the
marines. I had to knock out one of
Nasco's lackys just so that I could radio
5 Coy to tell them that the cure was
down there.

**Are you saying that Nasco didn't
want 5 Coy to be cured?**

Yes, they were all supposed to die.

**Were there any reprisals for
disobeying Nasco?**

You could say so - the 'lacky' decided
to reshape the points of my ears, and
Nasco sent me to the infamous research
station on X243.

**What was the nature of the
research carried out there?**

It was a survival equipment testing
station - we would test these things out
before they were released to the
marines. When I arrived they had just
commenced testing a range of exo-suits.

**Why did they call it the
slaughter-house?**

Because that's where Nasco sent
anyone he didn't like to die in
equipment testing 'accidents'.

**Why did people there let it
happen?**

You have to appreciate the situation -
paranoia was rampant, you didn't know
who was in Nasco's pay and you
didn't know who to trust...

**What changed?**

save anyone else the same way - then

No-one was killed by the explosion -

survived. They caught 4. He then blew
up the rest of the station and bugged
out.

**5 Coy's reports mention the

They're damned quick, very powerful
and hungry, but thankfully not
nocturnal. They're not sentient, so I/R
isn't that dangerous to them, but they
only attack stationary targets, so you
have to keep moving. Unfortunately
some of us had to die before we
appreciated just how deadly they
were...

No - he was just known as the 'Old

There were more dangerous things to

quickly stamped on. This was a top
secret establishment run by Nasco's
hand-picked bully boys, and there was
no-one who could stand up for your
rights!
