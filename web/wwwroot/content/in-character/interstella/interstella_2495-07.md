### Parnell sacks PAE
_Filed by: Krystal Spyers - Heurls, July 2495_

Ehlana Parnell hit the headlines earlier
this month when it was announced that
she had fired her Personal Assistant
Exec (PAE) for abusing the priviledges
of his position.

A communique issued by Ms Parnell
shortly after the announcement states
"a complaint was received yesterday
about the actions of Senor Savotti del
Zambese, my PAE. It concerned threats
made by The Senor to a Mayfair Hotel
manager - specifically that an unofficial
UW boycott of the Mayfair chain
would be made unless all the top floor
suites were emptied for members of his
family. This complaint was verified by
vid clips, and was later confirmed by
the Senor.

This was an illegal act, and was a
blatant misuse of his priviledge and
power. Such actions are wholly
unacceptable for any person, let alone a
member of my personal staff. The
Senor is no longer in my service, and
will be banned from employment for
any person or organisation working
directly or indirectly for the UW."

As if the very public humiliation of his
sacking from Parnell's office were not
enough, The Senor now has to face
charges of blackmail, threatening
behaviour and abuse of trust.

**Campaign continues**

Two days after announcing the sacking
of her PAE, Ehlana Parnell visited a
Groombridge Refugee Camp on the
frontier world of Heurls.

Life at the camp has reportedly been
much improved after corrupt local
officials were removed from the
council, freeing up money and
resources that had been diverted from
the refugees. Ms Parnell heard how
several projects started in the last few
months to improve relations between
the locals and the refugees had
achieved excellent results. One of the
refugees said that Ms Parnell had even
joked with them about the hospitality
they had received from 5 Coy on their
visit to the planet at the beginning of
the year.

When asked about her views on The
Senor, Ms Parnell commented that
corruption was at the very top of her
agenda - both corporate and that of
public officialdom.

One of Ehlana's election rivals - the
Honoured Noble Zachery Nathan-Swift
- visited one of the Refugee Camps on
Dawn. His trip met with the what has
become characteristic mayhem, as local
youths capitalised on the opportunity
to publicise their disapproval of the
camp by provoking some of the
younger refugees into a fight. Nathan-
Swift's Sec Team were kept so busy in
all the excitement that they were unable
to prevent him from being pelted with
ox dung.

### Supralight Communications
_Filed by: Professor Gerald Goldsmith - SOL, June 2495_

Following the recent comms problems
in outlying systems, Interstella! asked
Professor Gerald Goldsmith of the
University of Europe about potential
causes...

"Everyone knows about Radio - it has
been the main method of long-distance
communications used for centuries.
Regardless of the frequency used, the
background and mechanisms used for
Radio have hardly changed since the
20th century.

However, as man went out among the
stars, the limitations of lightspeed
restricted communications caused
increasing problems as it took longer
and longer for communication to take
place.

When Henri LeGuerre created the first
jump drive, his research, coupled with
that of Einstein and Hawking opened
up the possibilities of faster-than-light
(FTL) communications.

Now, FTL-Comms allow
communication between star systems
with no more delay than is experienced
when communicating between Earth
and Mars using normal Radio.

This was achieved by effectively
"encapsulating" compressed packets of
information within a jump field, and
using the same mechanism that allows
starships to travel FTL to transmit the
information. When the FTL packet
emerges into real-space, it reverts to its
traditional electromagnetic nature and is
received as a normal radio signal.

This would seem to be problematic -
anyone who has travelled on a starship
knows that how only short jumps can
be performed, and the exact point of
emergence is not known. However, as
the information packet has such a small
mass/energy signature (compared to a
starship, that is!), they can be sent
further, and at much higher effective
DF values - typically as high as 0.9 -
which would be impossible for a
starship to achieve. And because the
mass/energy signature is so small, the
emergence position errors are similarly
reduced.

In practical terms, this means that whilst
a starship might take 4 jumps at a DF
of 0.45 to travel from Earth to Alpha
Centauri, taking almost a week, and
THEN have to spend a week at
sublight flying into the system, a
message packet can be transmitted at a
DF of 0.9, and emerge within the

stories from around the systems follow:

**GIANT RABBIT EATS
SHOPPING MALL**

Following the nuclear detonations on
Groombridge in April, a 100 meter tall
rabbit has been seen roaming the area.
The creature is said to have eaten
several Chidanian and Kasdunni
armoured vehicles, and several
buildings. This included a department
store which was being used by Kas-
dunni Military Forces as their Area

The CMC deny all knowledge of the
creature.

The Groombridge authorities have
denounced the story as a hoax created
by the Billabong Regional Government
to scare aid workers out of the area in
an attempt to secure more aid for their
own region.
