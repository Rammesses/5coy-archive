### I. R. EXPOSURE & THE U. W. MEDICAL SYSTEM

Despite Assertions from the UW Medical Directorate and the Fleet
Medical Division that the IR treatment system had no Side effects
or Toxic after effects. Over the last 6 months it has become
apparent that this is not the case.

An increasing number of deaths are being reported and a
heretofore experimental Fleet surgical procedure is now being
regularly implemented in cases of severe IR exposure.

It is not known wether the Bio Engineered source materials from
which the drugs are produced is mutating or wether the users
nervous systems are being Genetically altered or damaged by long
term exposure to IR or the IR treatment drugs.

A senior Fleet Surgeon Commander has been assigned to 5th Company
to undertake the Surgical procedures and conduct a research
project into the Effects of the treatment procedures under field
conditions.

The attached sheet details the mechanics of the revised system
of treating and monitoring IR Exposure under combat conditions.

### I. R. EXPOSURE, TREATMENT & MONITORING.

1/ All players will be issued with an IR EXPOSURE card which is
unique to them

It will be held by the Surgeon Commanders team between missions
and issued to the person to whom it refers prior to the start of
any combat mission and collected at the end of that mission.
Whilst on any combat mission it must be carried at all times in
a position where it can be found easily by any Med Tech carrying
out treatment.

2/ IR EXPOSURE cards will be loaded with a pre determined grid
of monitoring markers which will record an individuals Cumulative
IR exposure and tolerance during a mission and determine the
effects that the IR exposure has on that individual.

3/ The grid is based on current research and deviations from the
standard exposure grid are only made in the following cases.

> A) Individuals from high background IR Planets.
>
> B) New Recruits
>
> C) Individuals with less than 6 active combat missions
>
> D) Individuals with a record of repeated High IR
> Exposure
>
> Cases A to C improve an individuals tolerances
> case D is detrimental to an individuals tolerances

4/ As the level of IR absorbtion increases so will the severity
of the physical after effects affecting the individual exposed.

5/ The cumulative level of IR absorbed will also determine the
grade of MED TECH who will be required to treat the casualty.

6/ The actual methods of treatment for IR exposure have not been
changed it is only the casualty management that is altered as
follows.

> A) a Med tech attending a casualty should first find the exposure
> card and check the current cumulative IR exposure level, this is
> to determine wether they are qualified to treat the casualty, if
> they are then treatment continues as normal. If not then a Med
> tech of the correct grade must take over.
>
> B) At the end of successful treatment the attending Med tech must
> record the IR exposure by removing the next available higher
> marker. Once this is done the exposure card must be placed back
> in the casualties pocket.

This exposure monitoring system does not absolve all med tech's
from keeping accurate and full records on all casualties treated.
