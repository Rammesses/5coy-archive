### Dekard, R.

- **Name:** Rik Dekard
- **Homeworld:** Luna, Sol system
- **DoB:** 3 November 2473.
- **DoE:** May 2502
- **Current Rank/Assignment:** Marine, 5 Coy.

#### Background:

His father (Lloyd Dekard, an ecological engineer)is from New Zealand and his mother (Roma , a hydroponics specialist) is from Scotland. His parent met on a eco-reclamation project in the Ukraine and were married after two years, and soon after Zoe, Dekard's sister was born. Three years later, Rik was born. Shortly after his birth, Lloyd got offered a job with the Tycho Corporation to work on the Luna Water Extraction project. After consulting with his wife it was decided that it would be best if the family relocated.

- **Education:** Luna Colony school. Took a degree in Ecology science but failed it in his final year.
- **Previous Employment:** Mostly dead end jobs working in the hydroponics section of the base.

#### Service Record:

- **Postings:** 5 Company
- **Qualifications:** None
- **Medals:** None
- **Reprimands:** None

#### Medical Record:

All the usual child-hood injures. Been hospitalised three times due to a broken right leg, concussion and tonsillitis.

Recruit training delayed due to severe intestinal infection, Chau Sara, May 2502, which necessitated medevac during the mission.

- **Allergies:** Allergic to pollen, fur and feathers. These are due to the clean atmosphere of the Tycho colony.

#### Miscellaneous Material:

- **Criminal Record:** Single incident when as a student got into a fight in a low-gee illegal brothel at the Tycho base.
- **Reason for Enlisting in the CMC:** To see the universe, and to find somewhere that is less boring than the moon.
- **Ambitions:** Nothing earth shattering just to see things that no one else has seen. Also has an ambition to become a travel writer.
- **Fears/Phobias:** Suffers from Vertigo and has this thing about flying insects that sting.
- **Strengths:** Loyal to his friends. A lateral thinker. Has a strong sense of right and wrong.
- **Weaknesses:** Does not suffer fools gladly. No real sense of humour. Has a strong sense of right and wrong.
- **Likes:** Good food, good wine, and good company. Enjoys old earth literature such as the classic science fiction novels of the people such as Lin Carter and Hugo Gernsback. Also likes the old Earth plays of Shakespeare and Johnson.
- **Dislikes:** Pretensions in people. Needless frivolity. Quite a lot of vegetables. Thinks that people who have no respect for their environment are foolish and beneath contempt.
- **Beliefs:** He is a member of Alcheringa, the ecology movement come religion started by Billy Cleverman, an Aboriginal philosopher and thinker. Its basic tenant is that everything in the landscape has to have a purpose and a reason. Anything without recognisable purpose or reason must be integrated into the framework of the world lest it provoke the collapse of the universe. Nothing can be taken from a land without putting something back in its place. As he is a member of the religion he carries with him his totem - a small piece of quartz.
