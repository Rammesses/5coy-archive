### INSURRECTION ON HAL

_Our Reporter_

It has become apparent that the United Worlds (UW) are again
interfering in the affairs of non-member planets, in blatant contravention
of their own mandate, following reports of the continued military
intervention by UW forces on the Planet HAL.

Troops loyal to the elected Government of Hal are proceeding with
their repression of the Slaves Revolt, engineered by UW in conjunction
with the Colonial Marine Corps (CMC). The number of dead on both
sides are currently estimated at around 7.5 thousand.

In this second incursion UW Forces, namely 5coy CMC (The UW's
foremost ?trouble-shooters?), are reported to be directly involved in the
instigation of the current rebellion and of supplying training, arms and
equipment to the rebels.

Eyewitness reports indicate that 5coy CMC are also responsible for the
murder of a number of unarmed civilians in the vicinity of Ruaros, a small
rural community, although no positive proof has as yet come to light.

From all appearances it would seem that the UW has created its own
version of the historical 'Bay of Pigs', a fiasco that occurred during the
20th century on Earth.

When will UW learn that they do not have a charter to do as they please,
that they too are subject to the same laws and regulations that govern the
rest of humanity, 90% of which have come at their own instigation. It is
small wonder that groups such as the Brotherhood and the One true
Church continue in their struggle against UW's tyrannical rule.

LONG LIVE FREEDOM

### Marie Celeste

_Reuters_

Continuing controversy surrounds
the demise of the spaceliner 'Marie
Celeste'. Was the destruction of the
ship a direct result of interference by
personnel of the CMC or was the fate
of the ship inevitable? As a result of
Insurance claims, presently running at
1.8 million credits, a commission of
enquiry is being set up to investigate
the actions of all those involved and to
apportion any responsibility, blame or
praise.

Preliminary statements are not ex-
pected before April of '96.

### Parnell Fraud

Shares in Parnell Industries, hold-
ing corporation of the Parnell family
fortunes, fell for the third consecutive
day of trading as another 47 points
were lost on the Dow-Jones today.
Following revelations of large-scale
embezzlement from one of its main
manufacturing ecologies, total losses
are now estimated at around 197.5
million although informed sources think
that the final figure could be as much
as three times this.

Trading in Parnell shares was sus-
pended at the close today.

### Stop Press

Further to reports from Hal, we have
received the following from a freelance
reporter, Martina Campana, who was on
the scene during the earliest days of the
rebellion.

Two members of the Slave Security
forces (SS) 'in charge' of a female slave,
had bound her wrists and attached them to
a long rope. To pass the time, they were
making her play football ( a sport at the
height of it's popularity in the mid to late
20th Century) The half starved woman
was made to chase the ball only to be
yanked off her feet before she could reach
it. With temperatures soaring into the
ninety's, this poor listless creature, hun-
gry and thirsty, was taunted by the SS men
who often struck her for no apparent
reason, also offering her water and then
pouring it onto the sunbaked ground,
laughing cruelly. I could only watch in
helpless horror as finally, one of the guards
took pity on her and allowed her to rest
and have a drink but the other, then
heartlessly dashed the cup from her hands
before she could quench her thirst.

Thankfully, at this point some people
arrived, mostly troops, although I did
recognise one of them as being a shop-
keeper. He was outraged at the state of
this poor woman. He hit one of the SS
guards, knocking him to the ground. The
other guard appeared less sure of himself
at this turn of events and made a hasty
retreat, dragging his 'partner in crime'
with him. Using this diversion I made my
escape before I could be spotted.

During the night, I witnessed what
appeared to be CMC troops being routed
from their base camp by an impressive
display from units of Unified Forces regu-
lars. It was a rousing sight, the assault
being executed with the aid of a brightly
lit armoured assault vehicle flanked by
foot soldiers, to the sound of blaring
Martial music! UW's 'best' were put to
flight, causing them to abandon their
camp and equipment. I am sure that had
this scene been captured on video, it
would not have enhanced the CMC's
credibility!
