### Unrest on Sirius

Confused reports are emerging of
widespread unrest in a number of
districts surrounding the planets
main spaceport and other major
facilities.

The sudden death last week of
Armand De Seigneur, the 10th
Baron Lukel, in what are rumoured
to be bizarre circumstances, seem to
have been the trigger for these
disturbances.

Both Sirius and the Lukel family are
no strangers to intrigue. The 10th
Baron's eldest son attempted to
depose his father in 2492 by means
of a complex deception involving the
supposed underground dissident
leader the 'Viper'. The plot was
foiled by a company of Colonial
Marines.

Up until the time of this attempted
coup the planet had an appalling
civil rights record, most of the
population is made up of indentured
workers and the Lukel family rules
the planet in a ruthless manner, with
any form of dissension being
stamped out by their secret police.

In recent times things had started to
improve, helped no doubt by the
planets terraforming process having
reached a point where the surface
conditions had become only merely
unpleasant rather than dangerous.

However as the 'Government' of
Sirius has imposed its customary
news blackout, the reason for the
sudden eruption of violence is as yet
unclear. Sources close to the family
are speculating that the matter of the
succession could be at the heart of it.

The 10th Barons brother Roger De
Seigneur is believed to be currently
running the planet and is apparently
said to be on the point of declaring
himself the 11th Baron Lukel.

A statement from the External
Affairs Ministry confirmed that a
request had been received from
officials on Sirius for assistance in
controlling the situation and that a
company of marines from the Rapid
Response Battalion were being
despatched.

### DIPLOMATIC MISSION LEAVES FOR K685

Following the dramatic exodus of
the entire Solrebrian diplomatic
corps last month. News has emerged
from the office of the Minister of
External Affairs concerning the
imminent departure of a special
diplomatic mission to the Solrebrian
Homeworld.

In a statement delivered at a
hurriedly arranged press conference
earlier today, the minister for
external affairs the Honourable
Zachary Nathan Swift told reporters
that following intensive negotiations
with the Solrebrian ruling council an
agreement had been reached for the
establishment of a permanent
diplomatic legation on K685.

Full details concerning the
establishment of the legation And
who is to be named as ambassador
were not forthcoming. However a
list of the personnel leaving
immediately to establish the mission
was released.

That it contained the names of some
of the UW's most able diplomats
came as no surprise, but two names
on the list did produce a flurry of
questions.

The first surprise was the presence
of Tane Diamonds name in the list
as senior legation administrator.
Leading to immediate speculation
that her father was in fact the chosen
ambassador.

The second surprise was the
inclusion of a currently serving
member of 5th Company of the
Colonial Marine Corps' Rapid
Response Battalion: Sgt Ruby Axe.
The mystery surrounding the
inclusion of Sgt Axe was heightened
as it became clear that she was not
being shown as part of the legations
security detail.

When questioned about these
appointments all the minister would
say was that they had both been
specifically requested by the
Solrebrian Ruling Council.

The minister closed the press
conference by saying that he was
convinced that the establishment of
the permanent legation on K685
would enable the re-establishment of
full diplomatic relations in due
course.

Legation members leave for K685
later this month.

### "I'M NOT A RAPIST!"

**SAYS SENIOR TECHNICIAN ARNOLD**

We received a letter from Senior
Technician Arnold who is rather
annoyed about the way his behaviour
on the Marie Celeste was portrayed.

"I have recently returned to work
after the injuries I sustained aboard
the Marie Celeste. Since returning I
found that old friends will have
nothing to do with me and
relationships are generally strained.

"I have now discovered that the June
issue of Interstella referred to me as
a "Sadistic Madman" and the person
responsible for the rape of 2
Marines. It is obvious since Slug and
Housens were hospitalised with me
that only members of 5 Coy could
have given this story to Interstella.

"Since I have not been charged with
these very serious offences and have
no recollection of them it was unfair
and libellous to say I did, especially
as I could offer no defence being ill.

"I have taken legal advice and unless
I receive an apology for these slurs
against my name I will be taking
further action.

"The marines who died trying to
save me and the others aboard do
have my heartfelt thanks for their
sacrifice, as do all the marines
involved who did not talk to the
press smearing my good name"

OK Mr Arnold, you say it was unfair
that you did not have a chance to
defend yourself. I agree.

You say in your letter that you have
no recollections of committing the
serious offences. Does that mean you
didn't do it or are you saying: "I
Senior Technician Arnold am a
rapist with a bad memory"?

Now sir if I give you a chance to
defend yourself I must give the same
chance to those marines who are at
the other end of your liable court
action threats.

Ladies I understand how sometimes
you don't want to face up to an
awkward situation but you have both
sworn an oath to protect the people
of the colonies. Senior Technician
Arnold is one of those people you
have sworn to protect - The women
who are exposed to him are also
entitled to your protection.

If this man did not rape you it is
your duty to speak out and ensure he
is not persecuted. If he did rape you
it is your duty to shout at the top of
your voice: "This man is a rapist!"
and to keep on shouting until he is
locked up where he belongs.

I hope you will have the guts to do
what is right.

_BENJAMIN MAGUIRE - MAR 2496_

### Palmini Sells STAR WARS Planet Shield

Palmini Defence Systems Inc have
been commissioned to build a
planetary defence system.

Work has already begun on the
project but Palmini are still
advertising for additional workers.

Palmini have rented five new
factories across Barnards and have
set up a massive training course to
get its extra production lines moving
before the month ends.

Palmini issued a press release to
point out the difference between
their system and Earth's 'Star
Wars': "The system we are
developing is superficially similar to
the modified SDI or Star Wars
system used by the USA to defend
Earth during the Colonial War,
however their are many differences.

"The USA's SDI system was
designed to defend against the USSR
(A major nuclear power in the 20th
century - Ed) but not built until
almost eighty years later during the
panic of Earth's Food Wars when
the USSR was long gone.

"It was upgraded slightly to defend
against outside attack after the
Groombridge Bush War but the
other nations of Earth refused to
invest in SDI and the USA in turn
refused to expand it to protect the
planet.

"By 2297 when the Colonial War
broke out the system was 200 years
out of date. SDI was highly
dependant on hard to defend ground
based components.

"The Planetary Occlusive Defence or
POD system we are building is
100% space based. We're using the
best ideas from SDI and abandoning
the undependable things like X-ray
lasers and railguns.

"Proven technology we're using
includes: 100 megawatt lasers,
smart-mines and homing-and-kill
rockets launched from space
platforms.

"POD is a modern and effective and
will give total protection against
unauthorised space vehicles."

Palmini Defence Systems refused to
name their client or the planet POD
will defend, however most military
and political experts speculated that
the system would take over from the
navy fleet protecting Groombridge.
(See separate story - Ed)

A week after Palmini announced
the building of the system an
unexpected development occurred.
An organisation calling itself "The
Holy Defenders of Juliet" contacted
three different media companies on
Barnards and threatened that "If any
Palmini attack device is placed in
Juliet's space the people of Barnards
will pay for their crime in blood."

### Groombridge

It's eleven months since the UW
were persuaded to detonate nuclear
charges on the fault line of the
battered planet. Has it done any
good?

The Navy's 'protection' ships float
in orbit over the planet. The
scientists sit in their company's
space station and monitor the planet
below.

They report that the tidal waves have
stopped and the earthquakes have
fallen into the predicted pattern.

I tried to contact their head scientist
Professor Brewitt to ask when the
earthquakes would stop but was told
he was not available to comment.

He later sent the following
statement: "Phase one of the
operation pushed the earthquakes
into the correct frequency and in
April when Groombridge's moon
causes a solar eclipse directly over
the target area the gravitational
forces should push the planet into
phase two."

Further attempts to get a proper
answer from the science team were
all met with the phrase: "We have
no comment to make at this time."

This leaves us with several
unanswered questions:

Did the Groombridge salvage
company know that 'phase one'
would not stop all the earthquakes?

If not then how did the company
charter its evacuation fleet so
quickly?

What exactly is 'phase two'?

How many more 'phases' are the
Groombridge refugees expected to
wait for before they can go back to
their homes?

### STOP PRESS:

**Restyk Fined**

A run on The Rystyk Corporation
stock was fuelled by the UW when it
announced a punitive fine on Restyk.

been released but sources have said
it runs into hundreds of millions. A
UW spokesperson who announced
the fine did say the fine was for
grievous malpractice. The fine was
pending but further evidence of
malpractice has been discovered and
the fine has now been levied. No
arrests are expected. In the
immediate aftermath two directors at
Restyk have resigned from the board
but refused to say why. A Restyk
spokesman confirmed their
involvement in the business
malpractice but refused to comment
on what form this malpractice took.
Analyst think that Restyk will
weather this storm but the damage to
the companies reputation will take
years to recover
