### ARSON ATTACK DESTROYS ORPHANAGE

Arson is suspected, following a fire
which killed 68 children and three
supervisors.

The Orphanage known only as
Tipard Valley Estate Building 42,
caught fire at 11pm on March 10th.

Hearing the fire alarms sound the
supervisors woke the children and
they all hurried downstairs.

When they got there they found that
their exits to safety would not open.

Some of the trapped children
hammered in vain on the doors, as
others ran around the building
attempting to get out.

Two Nas-corp engineers, flying in to
investigate a power outage in the
Tipard Valley communications
uplink, finally raised the alarm.

"As we came over the hill we saw
the smoke over at the top of the
valley," said Bob Swinton the
shuttles pilot. "Alan immediately
called the emergency services. I flew
higher and nearer, to get a better
look at where the smoke was coming
from.

"We thought the upper end of the
valley were all warehouses, but when
Alan got through to the fire service
the operator asked us to check to see
if it was a warehouse or the
orphanage.

"As we got closer, we could see
people smashing at the windows
above the second floor. Flames were
already coming out of ground floor
windows. As Alan was telling the
fire service operator, I saw a woman
on the forth floor, knocking out the
security bars with a fire
extinguisher."

A Tipard Valley Estate security
guard arrived at the scene and
managed to unblock two exits. The
Nas-corp shuttle picked up nine
children who had been able to get
onto the roof.

Many children escaped from the two
opened exits or jumped from the
fourth floor but the 71 still trapped
inside were overcome by smoke
before the fire service arrived.

135 were taken to hospital suffering
from the effects of smoke or broken
bones.

_FRANKLIN COLLINS - AURORA - MAR 2496_

### Mr Spock found in Proxima Centaurai System!

No; not the half alien scientist from
the 20th century space entertainment
show.

2309 Mr Spock is an asteroid which
disappeared from its normal Sol
orbit during April last year.

The missing asteroid was spotted by
the SS Galah, one of Nordburg
Shipping's ships sent to search for
the Pride of Harfen.

The Galah was near Proxima
Centaurai VI when an uncharted
object appeared on their scanners. A
closer approach showed a starship
attached to one side of the minor
planet. The Galah immediately
dispatched its rescue shuttles.

The rescue teams found that the
ship, a 150 year old tow tug, was
deserted. All power sources were
shut down. The identification
beacons on the asteroid had been
deliberately disabled.

The Galah's crew repaired the
beacons and passed the ship's
registration number on to the
Colonial Salvage Agency, before
continuing on its search.

It is unlikely the asteroid was stolen
for its mineral content. Sigma
Mining has the rights throughout
Sol as well as Proxima Centaurai.

_FRED DANTON - PROXIMA CENTAURAI - MAR 2496_

### Sirius situation continues

The unrest on Sirius has not yet been
resolved. The news blackout has not
yet been lifted and no news has
emerged from the planet.

Part of the CMC's Rapid Response
Battalion was sent to the planet last
month.

However a CMC spokesman recently
refused to comment stating: "As an
associate member Sirius has the
right to resolve its problems in its
own way. We are of course assisting
but I cannot say more at this time."

### TERRORISTS ATTACK

Security forces have gone on alert
following a spate of explosions and
bomb threats.

The attacks started on March 21st
when a bomb destroyed an electricity
sub-station on Braconis.

Twenty six bombs have exploded so
far and the terrorists have made
more than twice that many false
calls.

The terrorists have not yet identified
themselves in their warning calls, or
made any demands.

So far the attacks have taken place
on all the core worlds, except Alpha
Centaurai, Beta Hydri and Rhalanor.

No bombs have exploded on the
outer worlds but no one has any idea
of the bombers next move.

Six people have been hospitalised.
Remarkably nobody has been killed.

_JILL HILTON - MAR 2496_

### Return To Groombridge

Military Mercantile sent a ship to
Groombridge this week. The ship,
however, was not returning to the
stricken planet.

Its mission: to retrieve items from a
research base sealed back in 2323.

The secret research base, on the
barren moon of the fourth planet,
was built early in the Colonial War.
It was used by MM's scientists, to
test new weapons, before they were
used by the Earth Loyal Forces.

Military Mercantile's engineers,
wearing space suits, re-entered the
base and removed all the documents
and equipment stored there.

The materials are to be relocated to a
new storage centre.

The spokesman who showed me the
empty site, before it was re-sealed,
refused to say why the materials
were being moved or where they
were going.

Experts say that the company's
actions suggest they have decided to
abandon the system for good.

_PETER DYSON - GROOMBRIDGE IV a - MAR 2496_

### Churchill Denounced

John Churchill's alien artefact
claims were dismissed as a money
making scam by leading UW
archaeologist James Dyer. He told a
press conference:

"Churchill was asked to leave the
UW's excavating team, after
documented finds that he was
responsible for went missing.

"The stolen items were later found to
be in the possession of a known
collector.

"Since his fall from grace Churchill
has been involved with 'archaeology
by moonlight' and has destroyed a
number of sites, to steal objects he
can sell on the black market."

_ANDREW STEWART - SOL 3 - MAR 2496_
