### CMC Special Forces are now wanted criminals

Under United Worlds resolution
21463 passed on 24th November
2495 all CMC Special Forces
personnel are stripped of rank and
are no longer authorised to act on
behalf of the United Worlds or any
of its member planets.

Special Forces personnel are to be
charged with various offences
ranging from being absent without
leave to treason and projectile
weapon offences.

All members of Special Forces units
are to be considered armed and
dangerous and should not be
approached under any
circumstances.

If you see anyone from Special
Forces leave the area immediately
and contact your local police force
who will arrange for a CMC unit to
deal with the threat. Do not re-enter
the area until you have been told it is
safe to do so.

### Lannor demands compensation from CMC

The Planet Lannor has demanded
compensation for the actions carried
out by CMC Special Forces in
November.

A large contingent of Special Forces
arrived early in November,
apparently with the intention of
taking control of the government
and forcing them to opt out of the
UW.

When CMC HQ realised what was
happening they blockaded the planet
and dropped 5th Company to attack
and destroy the renegade force.

During the siege which followed
members of the public were not
evacuated out of the area and were
exposed to the risk of projectile
weapons, as Special Forces
personnel ran wild kidnapping and
murdering civilians.

### UW MEDICAL TEAM FINISH WORK ON CONNET

The UW medical team dispatched to
the Little Dunnet-on-the-Marsh back
in March last year has pronounced
the area safe.

The team were called in by the late
commanding officer of 5th Coy (the
then Major Harris) to treat "an
outbreak of Marsh Fever."

They inoculated thousands in the
area for this "fever". Krystal Spyers
a reporter who was in the area to
cover Connet's application to join
the UW was also treated.

She said in Interstella! at the time
that tests on her blood showed that
she had in fact been treated for
Hansen's Disease (The virus
released by Nasco on Altair IV in
June 2491, wiping out most of the
population).

Surgeon Lieutenant Griffin, head of
the team, said at the time: "Ms
Spyers was treated for Hansen's
Disease back on Altair IV and any
tests done on her will of course show
Hansen anti-bodies. Of course we
will now have to allay the fear
spread by Ms Spyers and Interstella!
by leaving behind a team to monitor
the area.

I spoke to Surgeon Lieutenant
Griffin today, as her team packed up
its monitoring equipment and
prepared to be removed. She told
me: "The operation has been a
complete success. We have
monitored the people, the ground,
the water, the air and the plants and
animals for every day for nine
months. We have not found a single
sign of the new strain of Marsh
Fever, or indeed Hansen's Disease."

I asked her if her team was being
pulled away to treat people
elsewhere, but she refused to tell me
saying: "I'm not prepared to enter
into speculation about things like
that. It just causes unnecessary fear
and stress amongst the population at
large."

_JANE MORGAN - CONNET JAN 2496_

### STOP PRESS: Marie Celesta cover up?

We have just received the following
press release from Parnell Industries:

"On May 27th 2495 we received a
transmission from the Garavaglia II
requesting help in shutting down a
faulty JSE drive onboard the SS
Marie-Celesta. We immediately
diverted the SS Belaway and
contacted the Lumiere Navy, who
sent the LSS Assegai, as soon as
medical personnel could be loaded.

"The Belaway arrived in the early
hours of May 29th (Shortly after the
Garavaglia II left. - Ed.) and began
scanning the debris to search for
survivors or bodies.

"The Belaway's scans indicated that
there was not enough debris for an
entire ship.

"When the Assegai, with its more
accurate equipment, arrived at the
scene fourteen hours later they
confirmed that only a small part of
the ship could have been blown up.

"We believe that the Marie Celesta
is still partially intact. We think that
5th Coy and/or the crew of the
Garavaglia have some idea of where
the damaged ship went.

"We have rescue ships standing by
with technicians and medical teams
on board. Our crews may still be
able to save lives.

"We ask for the CMC and the Navy
to tell us the truth about where the
Marie-Celesta went before it is too
late to save any survivors."

CMC HQ said that they would look
into Parnell's claims but could not
make a statement at this moment.

A Hadins-Worthy Starlines
spokesman told us that falsely
raising the hopes of the relatives
killed in the Marie-Celesta disaster
was a cruel act on the part of
Parnell.

_LUCY PACKARD - LUMIERE - JAN 2496_
