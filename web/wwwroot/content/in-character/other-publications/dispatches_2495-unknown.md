## DESPATCHES. · CMC NEWS

::: column-left
**<u>DESPATCHES</u>** is intended as an
alternative form of communication for
all sections of the CMC. Within its
pages you may find official statements
of CMC policy, details of honours
awarded to CMC personnel, the
CMC's version of events that may have
been reported in the media, and hope-
fully much more!

Also included in this premier issue is
Quartermaster, which will keep you up
to date with the latest in CMC
technology and equipment, carrying
reviews of new or even existing items,
special offers that are exclusive to
CMC members, etc.

Contributions from all sections of the
CMC are invited.

### Drug Alert!

Large quantities of drugs and other
Med supplies are missing from CMC
stores on a number of planets.

Several items are known to have
appeared on the black market at
highly exorbitant prices, but with their
quality seals broken. It is suspected
that drugs are being 'watered' down,
and any equipment obtained from
such a source can no longer be relied
upon.

Any items which are suspected as
having been stolen from CMC stores
are to be reported to your
Commanding Officer at the earliest
possible opportunity. Failure to
comply with this order will result in
disciplinary charges

### Comms

Reports have been made of suspected
breaches of comms recently. A Code
Two must be reported if comms
security has been compromised.

### Groombridge -

Every member of the CMC must have
heard by now of the worsening
political/religious/civil unrest, that has
broken out on Groombridge.

It is with regret and our deepest
sympathies for their families, that we
have to report that 7 more marines
have now died as a result of this
conflict.

The Roll of Honour is as follows:

- Lt Sgn B.C. Baccahrat
- Sgt A.O. Kyoke
- MFC S. Willis
- MFC Broker
- Marine C.K. Brown
- Marine Narochov
- Marine G. Billings

This brings the total number of
fatalities amongst 4 Coy to 12.

_They will not be forgotten..._
:::

::: column-right
### Diplo Team

Thanks to the combined efforts of
CMC Special Forces and volunteers
from 4 Coy, four members of the
UW Diplo Team posted to the
Chidarian region of Groombridge
were rescued after a brutal kidnapping
attempt.

It is hereby confirmed that The Head
of the UW Diplo Team - the Lord
High Keeper Alemco Drahvet - is
the father of Lt Sgn Diamond,
formerly of 5 Coy.

### Torboran Haze

Following recent intense media
attention, no member of the CMC
shall reveal the whereabouts or details
of the civilian Torboran Haze. No
comments must be made to the media,
whom should continue to be treated
with respect and courtesy.

### Survey Team

By now the entire universe must be
aware of the intense speculation
surrounding media reports of the
discovery of alien artifacts.

It is hereby confirmed that a squad of
CMC marines has been dispatched to
the planet, to investigate these claims
and provide support for the survey
team. It is stressed that reports of the
Survey Team having gone missing are
unconfirmed.

### Proceedings

A verdict has been reached following
disciplinary proceedings taken by the
CMC against Lt O'Shaunessy, 2 Coy.
She was found guilty of gross
professional misconduct and
malpractice, and membership of
LAW.

O'Shaunessy has been dishonourably
discharged from the CMC.

### Postings

Both 2 & 6 Coy have been posted to
Groombridge, to assist 4 Coy during
the current troubles.

1, 3 & 5 Coy have been posted to
(different) unnamed locations.

4 Coy remain on Groombridge.

7 Coy remain on stand-by.

**<u>PERSONNEL:</u>**

It is hereby confirmed that Lt Katz of
5 Coy has been withdrawn from
active service.
