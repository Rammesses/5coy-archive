### Training Bulletin

1. The following press release is reproduced by permission of the UW central press office.

**TRAINING COMMAND ESTABLISHED**

In a surprise press conference today, the United Worlds Joint Services Command unveiled their most
recent project - establishment of a central authority to oversee the training of all United Worlds Armed
Forces Personnel. The unit is to be known as **'Joint Services Training Command'** (**JSerTCom**) and
will formally commence operations on 09 May 2496.

Recent years have seen growing public and political concern relating to military training. This concern
has centred on expenditure in general, as well as several more recent training fatalities. At present,
most training is conducted and supervised at unit level, leading to the criticisms of poor
standardisation, unnecessary expense and unsafe training procedures.

The establishment of **JSerTCom** is seen in some circles as being merely a hollow gesture to public
opinion. Critics argue that the unit will be under funded, and that this, combined with inter service
rivalries, will result in a lack of effectiveness.

However, Rear Admiral Lawrence Clarke, JSerTCom's Commanding Officer designate, maintains that
this is not so. When challenged at the press conference he made the following statement:

> "Ladies and Gentlemen, my mission at JSerTCom will be to merge and rationalise the
> training procedures and resources used by the various different UW services. In many
> areas there is great duplication of effort from one unit to another. My primary aim is to
> identify these areas and then redirect the effort, both in terms of funding and manpower,
> to more productive ends.
>
> Subordinate commanders will be tasked with the creation of a training plan for their
> units. These plans will, upon approval, form the basis for the allocation of training
> resources. It is also intended to provide all units with support so that in house training
> can continue, but with significantly tighter standardisation.
>
> In response to the criticism of inter service rivalry in my department, I say this. In my
> experience the overwhelming majority of United Worlds Military personnel recognise the
> professionalism of their colleagues in their sister services. When such people are
> brought together the result is not petty squabbling but an operational synergy resulting
> from the breadth of experience. As a result it is my contention that my units 'Joint
> Service' designation is going to be one of its major strengths rather than a weakness.
>
> To summarise, a combination of inter service co-operation and an increase in efficiency
> will result in an improvement in the quality of our armed forces. The taxpayer has the
> right to expect that we should give the best defence possible with the available funds,
> both I and my team at JSerTCom are looking forward to playing our part in this process.
> In short ladies and gentlemen, watch this space!"

2\. This unit has received an Op Order from JSerTCom detailing its structure along with an
overview of the timetable for the implementation of new training procedures. The Op Order is available
for all personnel to view, contact me if you wish to see it.

3\. Part of the procedure involves a requirement to hold an Instructors Rating in order to conduct
official training courses. 'Ad Hoc' training for qualifications will not be recognised after the cut off date,
20 May 2496.

4\. There is a requirement for personnel to become officially qualified as instructors under the new
scheme. Initially, personnel having experience in a training role are invited to apply for an acting
qualification as Instructor level 4 (Assistant Instructor) and level 3 (Qualified Instructor). This will be
followed by the provision of instructor training courses, to allow acting instructors to confirm their
appointments, as well as the training of new instructors.

5\. Appointment as an instructor carries an increase in pay grade, opens up new career
opportunities and is the mark of someone committed to the service. All suitably experienced personnel
are encouraged to apply. This should be done by writing to JSerTCom 1 (Instructor Training Cell)
before the cut off date. The letter should detail your training experience as well as current
qualifications. You should pass the letter through the Commanding Officer who will endorse it with a
recommendation. The cut off date for applications is 16 Apr 2496.

6\. As part of the JSerTCom start up procedure, I have to confirm the current qualifications of all
personnel in the unit, by the same cut off date, 16 Apr 2496. You can do this either by updating your
entry on the Ops personnel database or arranging an interview with me. Qualifications not confirmed
by that date will be lost, you will need to retake the course examination in order to requalify. You have
been warned.

_Damien De Lima_ (signed)

### Company Routine Orders

1. During the last mission there were several occasions when individual personnel left the base
   area without authorisation.

2. The results of these uncoordinated actions may well have compromised the security of the unit
   and the success of the mission. As a result, in some cases, disciplinary action is underway.

3. All personnel are reminded that they may only leave the defensive perimeter under the
   following circumstances:

    - When specifically authorised to do so by the Commanding Officer, or in his absence the
      senior rank present inside the perimeter.
    - When in hot pursuit of hostile forces which have engaged the unit.

4. Failure to comply with these conditions is a serious violation of operational procedures and will
   result in disciplinary action.

5. Otherwise, the performance of the unit was well up to standard during the last mission. Your
   efforts in what were very trying circumstances were noted and appreciated.
