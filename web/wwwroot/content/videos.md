Footage shot by players at the events, and an in-character news special made afterwards. Most of it is from the Altair IV weekend, filmed from both sides: Krystal Spyers' news crew trying to get the story, and the marines of 5 Coy trying to get them off the base. Krystal Spyers' print version of that weekend is the [Interstella! Special Edition, December 2491](/article/interstella/interstella_specialedition_2491-12).

The videos were collected in an ex-player's YouTube playlist, "5 Coy Colonial Marine Corp": [https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL03E89988CFDFD0B7](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL03E89988CFDFD0B7) _(link checked 25 Nov 2026: still live, same content)_. Krystal Spyers Report 3 isn't in it.

Each video's page has notes and a transcript taken from YouTube's automatic captions, which are uncorrected and often mishear names.
