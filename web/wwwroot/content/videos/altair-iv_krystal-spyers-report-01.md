#### Notes

Krystal Spyers (the captions hear "Crystal spars") opens from Altair IV, where a plague has broken out. Rumour has it the marines released it as a chemical weapon against the Brotherhood, and she calls 5 Coy beaters, slaughterers and violators of human rights.

The crew films a firefight, is fired on, and names Lt Gill as the officer in charge ("leftenant Gil"). The marines order them out of the compound for their own safety, the crew insists it walked in through an open gate, and films a dead body. A marine called Steele is heard as the crew is pushed out ("Ste ... steel").

She signs off saying 5 Coy is the last company left on Altair IV while the whole base is evacuated, for the Skeleton News crew ("skeleton new crew").

The print account of this weekend is the [Interstella! Special Edition, December 2491](/article/interstella/interstella_specialedition_2491-12): the open gate, the corporal killed by a booby trap, the lost cure, the rendezvous and the evacuation.
