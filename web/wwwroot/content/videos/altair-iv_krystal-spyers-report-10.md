#### Notes

With 14 minutes before the dropship arrives, the security man is told he'll be treated fairly if he comes quietly. Later Krystal Spyers reports that her cameraman has been killed by the marines (the Special Edition says Sal "never recovered"), and films the body of one of her security guards.
