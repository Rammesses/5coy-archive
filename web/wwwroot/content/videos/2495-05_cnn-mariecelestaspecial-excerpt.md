#### Notes

A cut-down CNN special made after [Boarding Action - Marie Celesta - May 2495](/mission/2495-05_boarding-action-mariecelesta). The narrator says the starliner Marie Celesta went missing in August 2494, was found adrift in May 2495 by the CMC transport Garavaglia II, and was destroyed 36 hours later. It killed almost all her crew and passengers, and three marines. The captions hear her owners, Hadens-Worthy, as "hanesworth Spaceway".

The Garavaglia II is described as a light cruiser, one of the fastest ships in the fleet, and 5 Coy's transport, orbital cover and first line of support in the Rapid Response Battalion. The flight-recorder sequence follows the jump emergence, the contact (bearing 315, about 100,000 tonnes, running cold), a "type three freighter" whose ID beacon reads CSR 61402 3-9 Marie Celesta, and a probe scan showing high radiation, open sections and 125 life signs. The bridge dialogue follows the scenario's [Audio Script](/article/scenarios/mission-welcome-to-the-marie-celesta-audio-script) for the jump emergence.

It closes on Slug, named here as Robert Anderson, who made a fortune on talk shows and opened a chain of health farms selling "high rad" treatment. [In the Line of Fire](/article/in-the-line-of-fire/itlof_2495-06) reported 264 lost.
