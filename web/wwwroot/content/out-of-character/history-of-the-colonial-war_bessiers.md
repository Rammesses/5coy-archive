### Historical Outcome of The Battle:

The large scale missile strike failed to achieve the degree of
penetration expected.

Earth Force commanders had underestimated The Bessieres missile
unit commanders and technicians. Technically the PAAS system
operated much better than had been predicted and the deployment
around Appleby was very well planned and all units had full
missile loads.

The earthforce mission planners had also not allowed for the fact
that the extreme terrain around the target area would force the
missiles to gain height early therefore showing themselves to the
PAAS units radars much earlier than had been predicted.

This enabled the Raxe missiles to intercept at a much greater
range and in the event of a missed interception, the PAAS’s units
had time for a second launch against the incoming missiles.

The ground engagement was close fought with the Terran advance
being stopped along the NW-SE ridge 5km from Appleby.

Both sides took heavy losses.

However secondary supply depots spotted from the attacking
force’s position on the ridge, suffered extensive damage from the
Terran’s mortars.

And fate still had one tactically unexpected hand to play!. A few
hours after the mainly unsuccessful missile strike and without
warning! the main munitions storage area of the Appleby supply
depot exploded!!.

The resulting secondary explosions and firestorm destroyed the
entire depot. It was the Bessiere’s main munitions store.

It was later revealed that an apparently dud missile had landed
in the last minutes of the strike and in the confusion had been
ignored!!.

The Bessieres commander despite being killed, was criticised for
failure to remove the supplies to a safe location once the
missile had landed and failed (initially) to explode.

The missile strike and subsequent land attack was tactically
inconclusive. It did cause further loss of both men and materials
to both sides. and despite the missile that destroyed the
munitions depot this engagement only served to lengthen the war
on Bessieres considerably.

If the supplies had not been destroyed the Terran forces would
have been forced to surrender much earlier, possibly saving the
planet from planetary bombardment in the latter stages of the
war.
