### Groombridge

- **System Name:** Groombridge
- **Planet Number:** 150.3
- **Planet Name:** Groombridge

#### Data:

- **NVR Rating:** 9.0
- **H/R Rating:** 20.0
- **Atmosphere:** 2.0
- **Gravity:** 1.0
- **I/R Rating:** 3.0
- **Flora/Fauna Rating:** 3.0
- **% Water:** 70
- **Population:** 800,000,000
- **Diameter:** 3,000

#### Government:

Democratic, loosly based upon the
former United Europe, with an Upper,
Central and Lower House, elected
prime offical, and 5 main parties:
the Groombridge Democrats (GD), the
Evolution Party (EP), the
Groombridge Puritan Party (GPP), the
Future Party (FP) and the Communist
League (CL).

#### Main Corporations:

Network Systems and Military
Mercantile have both established
their Head Offices here.

#### Economy:

Communications, military equipment,
and tourism.

#### Military:

Large, efficient local police
force, small full-time and large
reservist army, several companies of
Colonial Marines, and corporate
security forces.

#### Brief History:

Discovered by the St Christophe and
the Tasrun in 2075, the planet was
claimed for the United Europe (UE)
by the St Christophe after the first
recorded case of space combat, and
was immediately colonised. In 2125,
the Groombridge Bush War broke out,
a territorial dispute originally
between the UE and Japan, but later
joined by several corporations. The
conflict was finally 'won' by UE
forces, supported by Military
Mercantile, but had resulted in the
deaths of millions and the drastic
contamination of most of the
planet's resources. Groombridge
supported Earth during the Colonial
War, and suffered further heavy
losses. Since the latter half of the
22nd century, Groombridge has
prospered, and its population has
rapidly expanded despite the
dangerously high radiation levels
affecting most of the planet.
