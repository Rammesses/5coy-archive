### INCAPACITATING RADIATION

To give us an opportunity to increase role playing in the system
below are the signs and symptoms shown by a person taken down by
incapacitating radiation (I.R.).

Signs and symptoms are similar to those of shock and should be
described by the player as the medic checks them :-

> Cold and clammy skin  
> Slowing pulse  
> Injury Diagnostic Program (I.D.P.) gives a reading

If the person is not down from I.R. when a medic does the I.D.P.
the player should then tell the medic that the reading is zero.

### SCREENS AND POINT BLANK RULE

After much discussion we decided that some points needed
clarifying and came to the following conclusions :-

1. The military screen works by holding on to 5 shots of I.R.
   \- when the 6th shot hits, the screen cannot hold any more and
   therefore the 6th hit goes straight to the wearer.

2. The civilian screen works by storing 5 shots of I.R. - when
   the 6th shot hits, the screen dumps the I.R. from all 6 shots
   into the wearer. Hence the person is killed immediately.

3. In the case of the point blank rule - the weapon has
   discharged inside the protective field of the screen - so much
   I.R. has been fired that the screen cannot deal with it - So the
   screen does not shield the wearer who receives all of the I.R.
   from that hit.

When a medic comes to revive the victim the number of hits
shown on the screen is the screens true status.

### GOING DOTTY

After running the spot system for a year most of you seem to
think that it is working. So now for the fine tuning.

Symptoms occur as that colour dot is removed i.e. if a green dot
is removed then the player develops a severe headache.

| | Dot | Effect | Treatment |
| - | ---- | ------ | --------- |
| 1. | **Blue** | No effect | Treated by any Med Tech |
| 2. | **Green** | 5 minute headache | Treated by any Med Tech |
| 3. | **Orange** | 10 minute - any or all of the following (depending on what you wish to role play): nausea, vomiting, dizziness, disorientation, generalised weakness | Treated by Med Tech 3 or above |
| 4. | **1st Red** | 30 minutes - as for orange but more severe | Treated by Med Tech 1 or above |
| 5. | **2nd Red** | unconscious | Treated by Med Tech 1 or above |

The colours of the dots you start with at the beginning of each
mission may now change. On that note see you in the new year.
