## DARK HORIZONS

## The Colonial Marine Corps

**Have YOU ever dreamed about what would happen if....**

- YOU had command of the Marines in Aliens
- YOU stood between humankind and destruction
- YOU had the resources of the 25th century

**Now here is your chance to find out. Join the
CMC and it will be YOU, not some actor or
storybook character, who has to get the job done
and get home alive!**

### So who are we

Dark Horizons is a professional live role-playing group,
dedicated to providing the best in interactive alternate
reality. For 48 hours, we ARE the marines on whichever
planet we've been sent to, some time in the future. Each
player has their own role, their own job to do. Sometimes
you will be given orders, sometimes you will have to work
things out for yourself.

### NOW HEAR THIS, NOW HEAR THIS...

Nobody in his right mind listens to a recruiting
sergeant, so the Marines asked me, an ordinary 'grunt' to
tell you a little about who we are and what we do. I have
been a Marine for about one and a half years, and I'm the
first from my planet' CoedArian, to join up since we
became part of the united worlds at the beginning of 2493.

I am a member of 5th Company, 4th Field Regiment CMC, the
spearhead of the Immediate Response Battalion, and we are
sent in first! Naturally we get the hardest jobs...why, I
remember on Groombridge last year [CENSORED...CENSO] but
most of us got home in one piece.

Don't be scared, you will not be thrown in at the deep
end. We train you first! To do this, we take you to
somewhere safe, some backwoods planet where you won't see
action until you are ready for it. There again, when I did
my basic training, we were attacked by the Brotherhood [a
bunch of interstellar terrorists] And had to retrieve a
satellite with a nuclear self destruct all poised to go
bang....the best laid plans of mice and men, as some Old
Earth poet said many centuries ago.

But it's fun most of the time, except when you are scared
stiff. And that does happen, believe me. No matter that
there's a drop ship waiting to pick you up, medics poised
to resucitate you....and the ultimate consolation that the
Marines will always get you...or your corpse...back!

RIGHT, LISTEN UP, the Marine who wrote the above is
already under investigation for talking too much. Be that
as it may, she's about right. Life in the Marines is
tough, but as rewarding as hell. You will really feel that
you have achieved something, when you pass off the square
on completion of your basic training.

### Back to the 20th century....

If you have what it takes to be a Marine in the future,
the here and now is easy! We at Dark Horizons invite you
to come and join us for a weekend full of excitement and
adventure. We will transport you forward in time, equip
and train you, allow you to prove your worth to us and to
yourself. And if you like it-come back for more.

Missions last 48 hours, and run approximately once a month
at various sites around the country. In the company of
experienced marines you will enjoy a fully catered
weekend, with plenty of excitement. All you need is a pair
of boots and a sleeping bag. We train you and provide
everything else you will need. The combat system is based
around infra-red eye-safe lasers, but that's not
important. All kit is supplied, we'll train you to use it.
The main thing you need is imagination, we supply the
rest, experienced marines, medics to heal you, bad guys to
fight... But this is more than combat simulation, each
player has a character with a past, a present and
[hopefully] a future. You can tell from the comments of
the Marine reproduced above just how real it can get. When
you come along, ask the experienced marines, they will
have a wealth of stories about past missions and their
bravery during them [some of them lie a lot!]

### Back to the Marine....

I can't really put into words the high of succesfully
completing a mission and getting back in one piece.
Civilians can't understand us, guess many think we're
crazy. Most of us can't wait til it's time to get back
into the drop ship and head off on the next mission.

A word of warning: this game is addictive! If you come
along, you WILL be back for more. Once you are part of the
Marines family, you will be sent details of future
missions, you will be able to read of your exploits in our
newspaper Interstalla!. As you continue to adventure with
us, you may be promoted - all of us started as raw
recruits, even the officers! - you may win medals, become
famous.. or stand court martial for doing something out of
line!. But you decide what you will do. Your dream, our
shared reality.
