### 1C01 AUTHORISATION

#### Command Authorisation Panel

::: figure-left-small
![1C01 Authorisation panel - palmprint scanner, access lights, control panel enable/disable and code lock keypad](/content/out-of-character/prop-designs/prop-designs_1c01-authorisation.png "1C01 AUTHORISATION")
:::

This panel controls access to the command override panels
by use of both palmprint recognition and personal ident
codes.

**Operation:**

The panel is activated by tabbing the upper right hand corner of the panel border.

The user then places his palm over the scanner for identification. Following scanning,
either the ACCESS AUTH tab will light, or the ACCESS DENIED, dependant upon the
user's authorisation status.

A further personal ident code may be required to release access to the controls - this is
indicated by the code lock tabs flashing slowly following palmprint verification. The user
enters his personal ident code or an override code and tabs the enter button.

The main panel controls are now released for access, allowing the user to enable or
disable the other command control panels. Further, a security warning may be set to
indicate any attempt at unauthorsed access to the command controls.

The user may also (if his authorisation allows) command the system to learn the palmprint
and personal ident of another person by use of the LEARN tab. The user presses the
LEARN tab, then the new user goes through the normal verification sequence.

The unit is disabled by tabbing the upper right hand corner of the panel border a second
time, or if the unit is unattended for 5 minutes.

### 1C02 MISSION STATUS

#### Command Mission Status Control Panel

::: figure-left-small
![1C02 Mission Status panel - status levels from Stand Down to Red Alert, siren controls and mission timer keypad](/content/out-of-character/prop-designs/prop-designs_1c02-mission-status.png "1C02 MISSION STATUS")
:::

This panel controls the Mission Status displays (1D01) and
the Mission Timers (1D02). It is used to signal status
changes, and to initiate alert states.

**Operation:**

The unit is enabled by tabbing the upper right hand corner of the panel border, if access
has been authorised via the Authorisation module (1C01).

The panel is divided into three sections, Status, Sirens and Timer.

The Status section on the left allows direct setting of Mission Alert Status by tabbing the
appropriate status level button twice (once to enable, then again to accept the override).
Additionally, external events may force an immediate alert status change, causing the
appropriate status level button to flash. The button must be tabbed to accept the new
status and re-enable the other controls on the panel.

The Siren section controls the alert sirens attached to this module. Normally triggered
automatically by alert status changes, the siren must initially be enabled by tabbing the
ENABLE key. The siren may be canceled AFTER the alert status has been accepted (see
above) by tabbing the CANCEL key. Additionally, the siren may be sounded manually by
tabbing the SOUND key, which requires use of the CANCEL key to stop the siren.

The Timer section controls one of the two Mission Timers. The first Timer displays local (or
Zulu) time, and may not be altered. The second may be preset to any value and may
cound up or down, allowing both the timing of missions (upwards timing mode) and time
before critical events (downward coundting mode).

The timer mode is set by tabbing the TMR (up) or CDN (down) keys and inputting six digits,
representing hours (2), minutes (2) and seconds (2), followed by tabbing the ENT key. The
ENT key accepts the new setting, and starts the timer in the appropriate mode.

Additionally, the second timer can be set to Zulu time by tabbing the ZULU key (which also
automatically sets the upwards counting mode.

A warning system is available for the timer countdown mode, This gives audible warnings
as the countdown progresses. The warninem is enabled by tabbing the ENA key. It's
unique siren may be canceled by tabbing the CAN key when it is sounding, or sounded
manually by tabbing the SND key. The voice messages accompanying the countdown is
Warning mode is enabled may not be canceled.

### 1C03 INTERIOR SCREENS

#### Command Interior Screen Control Panel

::: figure-left-small
![1C03 Interior Screens panel - status, siren, screen level keypad and IR status monitor graph](/content/out-of-character/prop-designs/prop-designs_1c03-interior-screens.png "1C03 INTERIOR SCREENS")
:::

This panel alows the control and adjustment of the interior IR
screens fitted to most ships and operational buildings.

**Operation:**

The unit is enabled by tabbing the top-right corner of the panel border, and disabled in the
same way.

The panel is divided into four sections, Status, Siren, Level and Status. The status monitor
is a large display panel on the right hand side of the unit and provides constant graphical
monitoring of the ambient IR levels and the screening the system is providing in a graphical
format.

The status of the screening system is controlled by the panel at the top left of the unit. The
system may be SHUTDOWN or placed ONLINE by tabbing the appropriate key.
Additionally, if a fault condition occurrs, the FAULT key will flash and must be tabbed to
acknowlege the condition.

The Siren section ENABLES, CANCELS or SOUNDs a siren which is linked to both the
systems fault detection circuits, and to a differential system whereby a dangerous
difference between then ambient IR level and the screening level provided is audibly
warned of.

The fourth section of the unit allows the operator to select MANUAL or AUTOmatic
operation by tabbing the appropriate key. Additionally, when in MANUAL mode, a pre-set
screening level may be entered using the 10 digit pads. The operator inputs four digits
which correspond to the screening level required. These digits are right entered into the
Screen LEvel field on the display, and come into effect when all four digits have been
entered.

### 7C37 E.B.U.

#### Emergency Beacon Unit

::: figure-left-xsmall
![7C37 E.B.U. front panel - status display (BCN, EMRG, SND, TX FAIL) and BCN, EMRG, ENA, SND keys](/content/out-of-character/prop-designs/prop-designs_7c37-ebu.png "7C37 E.B.U.")
:::

This unit provides a standard distress message generator
and planetary range transmitter in a single compact
package. The unit may be connected to additional
transmitter equipment to enhance range, and is fitted as
standard to CMC vessels and vehicles.

**Operation:**

Operation is entirely automatic under normal circumstances, the distress message and
beacon being transmitted upon command from the vessels command computer.

Additionally, the unit may be used in a hand-held mode with an additiohnal whip antenna,
giving a range of the order of a planetary diameter.

To manually enable the unit, press the ENABLE key.

The unit may be configured to transmit a distress message or a locator beacon, or both by
enabling either the BCN and/or EMRG keys.

Transmission is started by keying the SND key.

Transmission may be terminated by either disabling the unit with the ENA key, or the
transmitter/generator using the SND key.

If the unit is to be slaved to an additional transmitter, connection is made using the CON25
connector, leaving the RF connector unconnected.

### 4M01 RVC-877

#### Medical Diagnostic & Repair Package

::: figure-left-small
![4M01 RVC-877 Medical Diagnosis Unit (MediCare Research Labs, Dawn, Model MRL-877-02) - power, probe, yellow/green/red/blue keys and keypad](/content/out-of-character/prop-designs/prop-designs_4m01-rvc-877.png "4M01 RVC-877")  
![4M01 RVC-877 with probe storage](/content/out-of-character/prop-designs/prop-designs_4m01-rvc-877-probe-storage.png "4M01 RVC-877 - Probe Storage")
:::

The RVC-877 Unit is now widely used by the military for the treatment of incapacitating
radiation (IR). The unit was designed and built at MediCare research labs on Dawn and
was completed in 2488.

The RVC-877 unit takes the form of a square casing with a display screen centred by the
four control mode buttons. There are several models in circulation but all use the same
control sequence. The latest version is hand sized and reads the patients vital signs by
remote, however some of the earlier versions are larger and require sensor cables to be
attached to the patient.

**Operation:**

The unit has two usable modes and two further modes available for expansion. These
modes are the Screen Repair Program (SRP) and the Injury Diagnosis Program (IDP).

The SRP mode (Yellow control sequence) is designed to reactivate a military screen that
has absorbed or has been overloaded by incapacitating radiation (IR). The RVC-877 unit
sends the control sequence to the screen to allow the energy absorbed to be bled off.

Depress the yellow sequence control to select SRP and follow by the screen model
(current military screens use the model code of red). The RVC-877 will now start to decode
the military screen by showing the sequence to reactivate the screen. The sequence
shown should be copied by the operator until the RVC-877 has finished sending the control
codes.

The IDP mode (Green control sequence) is a program designed to scan the patient for
level of exposure to incapacitating radiation (IR) and assist in the diagnosis of the
resuscitant level.

Depress the green sequence control to select IDP. The RVC-877 unit will begin scanning
the patient and relay the information to the operator. To achieve the correct resuscitant
level for the metabolism of the patient, move the scan point across the display to route the
correct sequence. Finish the sequence by moving the cursor off of the display. The level of
resuscitant is shown by the digit indicated. If this model does not have such a display
then count from the top left corner (with the yellow control pointing up) to get an answer. An
answer in excess of nine should have nine subtracted from it.

### 4M74P MEDI-AID

#### Medical Sequence Storage Package

::: figure-left-small
![4M74P MediAid - log step display, FWD/RES/BAK and R/B/Y/G keys, power cells](/content/out-of-character/prop-designs/prop-designs_4m74p-medi-aid.png "4M74P MEDIAID")
:::

The 4M74P unit (commonly known as the Medi-Aid) is a coprocessing board for use with
the RVC-877 (4M01). It was designed by Landon Industries on contract to MediCare in
2491. The development of these units was never finished due to the liquidation of Landon
Industries though several pre-production prototypes were constructed. These units were
accidentally destroyed by Landon's security force when Landon Industries was infiltrated
by looters who, apparently, were attempting to steal electronic equipment. The 4M74P unit
was designed to remotely hook into any RVC-877 and assist in the reactivation of screens
while the SRP mode is in use. The unit scans and remembers the entire status of the
RVC-877 unit with each press of the control sequence controls.

**Operation:**

The digital display mounted at the top of the unit shows the current position in the
sequence. The seven controls are broken down into two sections, the data controllers and
the sequence controllers. The sequence controllers are used to step through the sequence
(both up and down) and to jump back to the sequence start. The data controllers are used
to enter the output from the RVC-877.

The operation is to enter the last part of the output to the unit and then to read them back
using the jump to start and then stepping through the sequence.

**Test Results:**

Trial runs showed that a skilled operator could use the 4M74P with a RVC-877 to give a
success rate of 92% with only a 11% increase in total operation time. While the sequence
time is slightly slower the improvement in success rate far out-weighs the time factor and
makes the unit extremely valuable to a field medical unit.

### 1C01 IR DOSIMETER

#### Personal I.R Dosimeter

::: figure-left-xsmall
![7M01 IR Dosimeter - display, LEV/TIM/CUM/DOS pads and instructions](/content/out-of-character/prop-designs/prop-designs_7m01-ir-dosimeter.png "7M01 IR DOSIMETER")
:::

The 7M01 Personal I.R. Dosimeter is a small, belt-mounted
unit that measures and displays both the current ambient
I.R. level, and stores the cumulative dose that the user has
been exposed to. Additionally, the unit can display the save
time remaining before the cumulative dosage becomes
dangerous to the health of the user.

**Operation:**

The unis is powered up and down using the slide switch on the right hand side of the
casing. In operational conditions, the unit should be enabled at all times.

The four front-panel pads select which measurement is displayed, these being Current
Ambient IR Level (LEV), Equivalent IR Dosage Level (DOS), Cumulative IR Doseage
(CUM) and Time Remaining Before Cell Damage Occurs (TIM).

Additionally, an audible warning sounds when the ambient IR level increases above a pre-
set value, indicating the presence of a 'hot-spot'.

_Printed on the unit:_

> 1: Power switch on RHS of Unit  
> 2: Select display of LEVel, TIMe to lethal dose, CUMulative dose or current DOSage level.  
> 3: Ensure unit is checked and calibrated by Medical staff every 28 days.
