**Other Publications** collects the smaller in-character news sheets that appeared alongside Interstella! and In The Line Of Fire:

- **CNN Newsline** (originally CNN Newsbulletin) - the Celestial News Network's broadsheet, 2496.
- **Despatches / 5 Coy Dispatches** - the CMC's own newsletter, and later 5 Company's orders and training bulletin.
- **The Voice** - an anti-UW, anti-CMC underground paper.
